# 0.1.0 Unreleased

## Features

* **The `plain` template.** The reference implementation of the template contract: an overview, a blog post built from the shared markdown fixture, a forms page built from the shared form fixture, a sidebar-layout variant, a 22-page docs tree (get started, tokens, defaults, blog anatomy, and one page per component with a live preview, copyable markup, variants, classes and notes), and a three-page example site. Two stylesheets, one 45-line script for the tri-state light/dark toggle, and a one-line `onclick` on dialog openers; everything else is HTML and CSS. Its palette block is a retoken theme.

* **The landing page.** `index.html` lists every template with a hand-drawn preview per scheme, a title search and tag filters (scheme, type, motion, fonts, feel) that combine, and links to each template's overview, docs and example site.

* **The `neumorphic` template.** Soft UI on a single surface: every card, button and field is pushed out of, or pressed into, the page colour by a pair of light and dark shadows, with no borders anywhere but a banner's coloured edge. Buttons and the feature card press in on click; inputs and the switch track are sunken. Cool grey with white highlights by day, charcoal by night; Nunito from Google Fonts. Example site: a smart-home dashboard.

* **The `brutal-news` template.** A brutalist newspaper: white paper and black ink, rules never thinner than three pixels, no rounded corners, hard offset shadows, and a harsh set of hues at full strength (hot red, acid yellow, cobalt, signal green). Archivo Black headlines, Inter body, JetBrains Mono kickers and data, all from Google Fonts. Flare: a masthead with dateline, ruled columns with a drop cap, tape, a ruled box and a red block. Dark mode prints the same paper in reverse. Example site: a local newspaper.

* **The `glassy` template.** Glassmorphism: a soft gradient backdrop built from three colour tokens, two blurred colour orbs drifting behind the page, and every card, bar, panel and dialog a translucent sheet with a bright edge and a backdrop blur. Pill buttons, large radii, a strip of four wave layers on a slow parallax that stops under reduced motion, system fonts. Example site: a small design studio.

* **The `neon` template.** Black zinc surfaces, one-pixel hairlines and two neon tubes: rose for actions and danger, cyan for information, each glowing through shadows rather than fills. The light half is only less dark: the ground lifts to a warm grey, the text stays off-white, links move to a lighter rose to clear 4.5:1, and native controls, dialogs and open popovers are pinned to the dark scheme so the browser never paints a white menu or scrollbar. The feature card's border is a conic gradient turned by a registered `@property` on hover; flare adds a breathing haze, lit text, a flickering sign and a glow utility, all still under reduced motion. Space Grotesk and Inter from Google Fonts. Example site: a status-page SaaS with home, pricing and changelog.

* **The `natural` template.** A field-guide look: cream paper with hairline rules, Fraunces headings, uppercase tracked kickers in moss and a rust accent, with a faint green tint in the page corner. The dark half is soil, a tiled SVG `feTurbulence` texture veiled by a `light-dark(var(--pal-bg0), transparent)` layer so the swap stays a colour, under cream and light-green text. The feature card is a pressed-flower specimen taped to the card with a paper tag that lifts on hover; flare is a ruled field note, a round nursery stamp and a leaf-sprig page break, all CSS. Fraunces and Source Sans 3 from Google Fonts. Example site: a plant nursery.

* **The `detailed` template.** Art deco: cream and bronze by day, navy and gold by night, with the navbar, top bar, footer and lightbox as black bands in both schemes. The overview opens on a marquee hero with an inline SVG sunburst behind a Limelight headline; corners are cut in steps with one clip-path token, the page break is a pair of gold chevrons, and the feature card wears a stepped gold frame and a gold-leaf sheen that sweeps across once on hover. Adds four namespaced variants (stepped card, gold outline button, menu table, marquee hero) and a three-page example site for a grand hotel.

* **The `gothic` template.** Pale stone and iron ink with a crimson accent by day; near-black with candle gold by night, since `#7a1f2b` cannot carry text on black. One pointed arch, a percentage `clip-path` token, shapes the hero picture, every gallery thumbnail, the avatar, the accordion marker and a `.card.gothic-arch` variant; everything else is square. UnifrakturMaguntia on `h1`, `h2` and the masthead only, Crimson Pro in small caps for labels, navigation and buttons; flare is an illuminated `::first-letter` initial, a manuscript page with marginalia, a rose window and an arcade rule, with the page break a quatrefoil, all drawn as `mask-image` tokens filled with a `light-dark()` colour. The feature card takes a stained-glass wash with lead lines on hover. Example site: a cathedral archive.

* **The `allegory` template.** Two faces of one site: by day ivory surfaces, an antique gold for links and a brighter gold for bands carrying white display type, wings around a halo and a soft radiance; by night off-black, a red for links and a deeper red for bands carrying black display type, horns around a flame and hard offset shadows, all from one palette block of `light-dark()` pairs. `.allegory-halo` and `.allegory-horn` pin any element to one face by setting `color-scheme` on it, so the overview opens on a diptych of both faces and the example's two chapter pages mirror each other with opposite bands; the ornaments are masks re-pointed on `data-scheme`. The feature card turns half a turn and shows the opposite face; flare adds the diptych, a display band, a verse and an italic vow button. Cormorant Garamond and Inter from Google Fonts. Example site: a story told twice, from a bell tower and from the well beneath it.

* **The `extraextra` template.** A poster wall: no maximum page width, sections ruled edge to edge with 2px lines in the type colour, and full-bleed bands in one bright orange (`--extraextra-orange`, the same in both schemes, always under black type) or in ink; paper and black by day, near-black and paper by night, with a burnt orange for light-scheme links so both halves clear 4.5:1. Anton in capitals for every heading, brand, button and badge, Barlow for the body, IBM Plex Mono for kickers, labels and data, all from Google Fonts; nothing has a corner or a shadow. The signature is a ticker of mono facts moving under the hero (paused on hover, still under reduced motion); variants are the poster card, shout button, set-times table and strip banner, and the feature card is a two-colour print that drops to one ink under an orange plate on hover. Example site: The Kiln, a music venue (home, line-up, tickets).

* **The `breathe` template.** A breezy green theme for plant apps: a white-green ground with large whitespace, white panels on soft shadows, mint as a surface token (never a text colour) and a deep leaf-green accent that clears 4.5:1; the dark half is forest, a blue-green, never brown. Manrope carries body and display with Instrument Serif italic for one word in a heading; pictures take a leaf cut (two corners round, two sharp) and the only motion is a frond in the hero that sways 2.4 degrees over seven seconds, stopped under reduced motion. Variants: `.breathe-leaf` on cards and fifty-fifty, `.breathe-stem` (a list of cards as a sequence with `-now`/`-done` nodes), `.breathe-quiet` buttons, `.breathe-mint` badges and `.breathe-week` tables, plus the frond, pot, stats row and aside as flare. Example site: Windowsill, a plant-care app (today, a plant profile, the week).

* **The `modern-frosty` template.** Near-white with icy blue tints by day and deep slate blue by night; hairline borders, corners of 4px and 6px, one cold blue for links, focus and the primary button, and frost (a translucent fill plus an 8px blur, two tokens) only where something sits over the page: the navbar, top bar, dialogs, lightbox controls, menus, the sidebar drawer and the feature card's pane, which clears on hover. Geist for text and Geist Mono for labels; no gradients, orbs or motion beyond hover. Signature flare is the note, a sticky frosted toolbar over a ruled sheet, used as the hero; variants are the ledger grid, the quiet button, the lined accordion group, the inline field and the plans table. Example site: Sheaf, a notes app (home, features, pricing).

* **The `thick` template.** Neobrutalism with bright yellow: every box has a 3px outline and sits on a solid offset shadow that collapses when pressed, so buttons, fields, thumbnails and the feature card visibly move. Light is warm paper with ink outlines; dark is near-black with the same yellow and off-white outlines. Rubik throughout (no other template uses it), tinted card and fifty-fifty variants, a flat button, sticker badges, a yellow CTA band and the pile, a stack of outlined sheets under a picture that is the signature. Example site: Paper Cuts, a zine shop (home, issues, about).

* **The `cattle` template.** Leather and wood: pale maple by day, dark oiled leather by night. Headings are branded into the board in Alfa Slab One with a scorched halo that bleeds into the grain, the navbar, sidebar, footer and author card are saddle leather with a running stitch, brass rivets sit on the switch and the accordion, a steer mark is the page break, and the feature card's title burns in on hover. Bitter for the body. Example site: Harlan Creek, a smokehouse (home, menu, book a table). Now on the landing page with hand-drawn previews and in the templates table.

* **A hero component in every template.** `docs/components/hero.html` documents the opening section with two variants: the template's own hero, as its overview uses it, and a second that moves and is only that template's. Plain's lines rise in turn under a rule that draws itself; Neumorphic's plate breathes; Brutal News slams the headline in stepped and stamps the corner; Glassy's discs orbit the words; Neon runs chasing lights around the stage; Natural's vine draws itself and unfolds; Detailed's curtains part; Gothic's candle flickers; Allegory's leaves lift in turn; ExtraExtra slaps posters onto the wall; Breathe's seeds drift up; Modern Frosty types its command; Thick drops the box and bounces; Cattle burns the headline in with smoke. All are CSS only, fill both ways, and stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The checker now requires the page, the contract lists the component, and every sidebar links to it.

## Bug Fixes

* **Neon's schemes swapped.** The zinc look that used to be the dark half is now the light half, and the dark half is true black: `--pal-bg0` `#141414` stepping up through `#1a1a1a`, `#202020`, `#2c2c2c` and `#3d3d3d`, with the same rose and cyan tubes on both grounds. The lifted grey light half is gone; nothing is white in either scheme, the rose clears 4.5:1 on both, and native controls stay pinned to the dark scheme. The palette block, README, theme page, overview copy and both landing previews follow.

* **Natural's soil texture made finer and quieter.** The owner found the dark-scheme dirt texture too large and too strong. The `feTurbulence` tile now runs at four times the base frequency, so the grain is a quarter of its former size, and the noise layers carry roughly a third of the contrast they had, so it reads as a faint grain in the brown rather than camouflage. Light mode is unchanged (the veil hides it).

## Other

* **Modern Frosty made more its own.** After the owner found it too generic, and with tasteskill.dev as the reference: the navbar is now the dock, a frosted pill that floats clear of the page edges and is the only thing on an ordinary page with a shadow; Newsreader italic joins Geist as a counterpoint for one phrase in a heading; the ink slab is a new inverted surface (text colour as ground) for a call to action or a command line with a blinking caret; one warm ember marks anything live; and the hero rises on load while the note beside it drifts. Four tokens added, the fonts link updated on every page, the landing card and docs reworded.

* **Structural checker and fixtures.** `tools/check.py` (Python, standard library only) enforces the template contract: required files, the head block, relative links, the retoken token rules with contrast maths, fixture and snippet parity, navigation consistency and the JavaScript allow-list. `fixtures/` holds the canonical markdown test article and form that every template embeds verbatim.

* **Checkpoint C tidy-ups.** `js/copy.js` shipped at 31 lines against the contract's 25-line budget; its header comment and two helpers were compacted to 22 lines in every template, with no change in behaviour. The theme docs page of Neumorphic, Brutal News and Glassy claimed the template had no tokens of its own when each defines several; the paragraph now names them.

* **Docs finished and copy cleaned.** `docs/user/using-a-template.md` and `docs/user/retoken.md` are written out (they were stubs), the root and docs READMEs list all nine templates and the distinctiveness rule, and the em-dashes plain's docs pages carried into every template (titles, list items, gallery captions, a few sentences) were replaced with plainer punctuation, following decision 21's copy rule; the fixture article is exempt.

* **Distinctiveness pass: Neumorphic.** The overview now opens on a working control surface (a dial, a segmented control, sunken wells and raised buttons) beside the headline instead of a headline-and-image hero, and the switch and forms lead the page. New namespaced variants: `.neumorphic-panel`, `.neumorphic-dial`, `.neumorphic-segment` and `.neumorphic-well`, each documented on its component page and on the flare page. The example is a home-control dashboard for a house on Larch Road (home, a room, settings).

* **Distinctiveness pass: Brutal News.** The overview is now a front page: masthead with dateline and edition, a lead story spanning columns with a drop cap, a ruled grid of secondary stories, a scoreboard table, folded briefs in the accordion. New variants: `.brutal-news-dateline`, `.brutal-news-byline`, `.brutal-news-pullquote`, the ruled story grid, the ruled box and the red block, plus a breaking banner and stamp button. The example is The Ashcombe Bugle, a local paper (front page, a story, subscribe).

* **Distinctiveness pass: Glassy.** The overview composes with depth: a sheet hero floating over the orbs and partly over the wave strip, cards in three depth tiers, a fifty-fifty whose figure sits behind the text panel, and floating and stacked glass utilities. New variants: `.glassy-shallow`, `.glassy-sheet`, `.glassy-deep`, `.glassy-pane`, `.glassy-float` and `.glassy-stack`, documented on the component pages and the flare page. The example is Osier, a design studio in Kendal (home, services and prices, contact).

* **Neon rebuilt as signage.** The overview and example site now open on a `.neon-stage` hero whose headline is tube lettering (`.neon-text`, striking once with `.neon-strike`) under a `.neon-marquee` fact strip, and the changelog is a dated `.neon-rail` with a lit lamp on the current entry. Component variants follow one rule, glow is state: `.card.neon-panel` with `-lit` and `-cyan`, `.badge.neon-light` state lights, `.data-table.neon-status` as a status board, `.btn.neon-outline`, the `.neon-sign` pill and a `.neon-glow` utility, each documented with a preview and snippet. The breathing haze and label flicker were removed so only the strike and the feature card's sweep move, both still under reduced motion.

* **Distinctiveness pass: Natural.** The overview now opens on a specimen plate (a large pressed-plant figure with a label block and Latin binomial) instead of a headline-and-image hero, cards are herbarium specimen cards with collection data, the table is a sowing calendar and the accordion a key. New variants: `.natural-plate`, `.natural-label`, `.natural-binomial`, `.natural-data`, `.natural-legend`, `.natural-sow` and `.natural-lift`, documented on the component pages and the flare page. The example is Fallow and Fern Nursery (home, a field-guide entry, visit).
