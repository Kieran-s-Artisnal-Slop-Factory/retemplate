# 0.1.0 Unreleased

## Features

* **The `plain` template.** The reference implementation of the template contract
* **The `neumorphic` template.** Soft UI on a single surface: every card, button and field is pushed out of, or pressed into, the page colour by a pair of light and dark shadows, with no borders anywhere but a banner's coloured edge.
* **The `brutal-news` template.** A brutalist newspaper: white paper and black ink, rules never thinner than three pixels, no rounded corners, hard offset shadows, and a harsh set of hues at full strength (hot red, acid yellow, cobalt, signal green).
* **The `glassy` template.** Glassmorphism: a soft gradient backdrop built from three colour tokens, two blurred colour orbs drifting behind the page, and every card, bar, panel and dialog a translucent sheet with a bright edge and a backdrop blur.
* **The `neon` template.** Black zinc surfaces, one-pixel hairlines and two neon tubes
* **The `natural` template.** A field-guide look: cream paper with hairline rules, Fraunces headings, uppercase tracked kickers in moss and a rust accent, with a faint green tint in the page corner.
* **The `detailed` template.** Art deco: cream and bronze by day, navy and gold by night, with the navbar, top bar, footer and lightbox as black bands in both schemes. 
* **The `gothic` template.** Pale stone and iron ink with a crimson accent by day; near-black with candle gold by night, since `#7a1f2b` cannot carry text on black.
* **The `allegory` template.** Two faces of one site: by day ivory surfaces, an antique gold for links and a brighter gold for bands carrying white display type, wings around a halo and a soft radiance; by night off-black, a red for links and a deeper red for bands carrying black display type, horns around a flame and hard offset shadows, all from one palette block of `light-dark()` pairs.
* **The `extraextra` template.** A poster wall: no maximum page width, sections ruled edge to edge with 2px lines in the type colour, and full-bleed bands in one bright orange (`--extraextra-orange`, the same in both schemes, always under black type) or in ink; paper and black by day, near-black and paper by night, with a burnt orange for light-scheme links.
* **The `breathe` template.** A breezy green theme for plant apps: a white-green ground with large whitespace, white panels on soft shadows, mint as a surface token (never a text colour) and a deep leaf-green accent that clears 4.5:1; the dark half is forest, a blue-green, never brown. 
* **The `modern-frosty` template.** Near-white with icy blue tints by day and deep slate blue by night
* **The `thick` template.** Neobrutalism with bright yellow
* **The `cattle` template.** Leather and wood: pale maple by day, dark oiled leather by night. 
* **The `termy` template.** A terminal. By day a dark green screen: near-black green ground, phosphor green text and accent, a glow on the letters; by night the same terminal printed black on white with a deep green accent.
