Hero

The opening of a page: a heading at the largest size, a paragraph, one or two buttons, and beside them whatever the template leads with. Every template has its own hero form; this page shows Breathe's, then two variants that move.

Variants

Text only

The same hero with nothing beside the words. Add hero-text: the grid becomes one column and the stack is capped at a readable measure. Every template carries this variant with the same class.

<section class="hero hero-text">
  <div class="stack">
    <p class="kicker">Words only</p>
    <h1>A hero with nothing beside it.</h1>
    <p>One column, the text measure capped so the lines stay readable, and everything else the hero has: the kicker, the heading at the hero size, the paragraph, the buttons.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
      <a class="btn btn-lg" href="index.html">Browse components</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

Image only

A hero that is one picture, edge to edge, cropped to a wide band. Add hero-image and give it a single <img> (or a <figure>) with a real alt, since there is no heading to say what it is. Every template carries this variant with the same class.

<section class="hero hero-image">
  <img src="../../assets/hero.svg" alt="The template's hero picture, filling the width" width="1200" height="500">
</section>

The seeds

Six seeds drift up from the bottom of the hero and out of it, each on its own slow path and its own delay, fading as they go. It is the frond's motion given to the air: nothing else on the page moves. Add breathe-seeds to the hero. Under prefers-reduced-motion the global rule cuts every animation to nothing, so the hero is simply there.

<section class="hero breathe-seeds">
  <div class="stack">
    <p class="kicker">A retemplate template</p>
    <h1>Let them <em>drift</em>.</h1>
    <p>Six seeds on six paths, nine seconds to the top, never in step. Each is a pale disc in a softer ring, and the ring is the mint token, so they belong to the page in both schemes.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
      <a class="btn btn-lg breathe-quiet" href="index.html">Browse the components</a>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="breathe-flare breathe-air" aria-hidden="true">
    <span style="--x: 12%; --d: 0s"></span>
    <span style="--x: 30%; --d: 2.5s"></span>
    <span style="--x: 48%; --d: 5s"></span>
    <span style="--x: 62%; --d: 1.2s"></span>
    <span style="--x: 78%; --d: 6.8s"></span>
    <span style="--x: 90%; --d: 3.9s"></span>
  </div>
</section>

The breathing ripple

Rings of mint spread from behind the heading on the rhythm of a breath: each swells slowly for most of its life and fades at the end, and the next begins before the last is gone, so the page seems to inhale and exhale. Nothing on the page moves but the rings. Add breathe-ripple to the hero; the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.

<section class="hero breathe-ripple">
  <div class="breathe-flare breathe-rings" aria-hidden="true"><span></span><span></span><span></span></div>
  <div class="stack">
    <p class="kicker">A retemplate template</p>
    <h1>In, and <em>out</em>.</h1>
    <p>Three rings widen from behind the heading on the rhythm of a slow breath: eight seconds each, a new one every two and a half, drawn in the deep mint and gone before they reach the edge.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
      <a class="btn btn-lg breathe-quiet" href="index.html">Browse the components</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css: the hero under /* == utilities == */ with the other layout helpers, the variant under /* == hero variants == */.

ClassPurpose
.heroThe opening section: a grid of a text stack and, in most templates, a picture or a piece of flare
.hero .stackThe kicker, heading, paragraph and button row
.hero .rowThe buttons
.hero.breathe-seedsThe hero clipped so the seeds leave at its top edge
.breathe-airThe layer holding the seeds
.breathe-air spanA seed; --x is its column, --d its delay
.breathe-dropThe drop
.hero.breathe-rippleThe hero with breathing rings behind its heading
.breathe-rings spanA ring; three, two and a half seconds apart
.hero.hero-textWords only: one column, capped measure (every template)
.hero.hero-imagePicture only: one image edge to edge, cropped 21:9 (every template)

Notes

  • One <h1> per page: the hero's heading is it. The kicker above it is a <p>, not a heading.
  • Above 48rem the hero is two columns, text then picture; below, it stacks. The variant keeps whatever layout its markup implies.
  • The variant is CSS only. Its animation is declared with both fill, so the page is correct before it starts and after it ends, and the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.
  • Another template ignores breathe-seeds and shows its own hero; any flare markup inside sits in the template's flare wrapper so a swap can drop it.
