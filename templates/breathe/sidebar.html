The other layout

The overview, with a sidebar

Every template ships two layouts. The navbar layout is on the overview, the blog post and the forms page; this page and the whole docs tree use the sidebar layout. On a wide screen the sidebar is a column of soft panels; on a narrow one it is a drawer behind the menu button, and it closes on Esc or a tap outside.

What the layout is made of

A grid with two rows and, above 64rem, two columns. The top bar spans both columns and stays put; the sidebar is a sticky column with its own scrollbar; the page scrolls under it. Below 64rem the sidebar carries the popover attribute, so the menu button is a real button that opens it, and the browser supplies the dimmed backdrop and the dismiss behaviour.

Groups fold

Each navigation group is a <details>, open by default. Close the ones you do not need.

The current page

The link to the page you are on carries aria-current="page" and sits on a mint pill, which is also what styles it.

How it is built
Can the sidebar collapse on a wide screen?

Not in this version. The drawer behaviour is for narrow screens only; on a wide screen the column is always there.

Does the drawer remember being open?

No. It is a popover: it opens when asked and closes when dismissed, and there is nothing to remember.
