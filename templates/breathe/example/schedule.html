How the week is worked out

Where do the dates come from?

Each plant has an interval, in days, and a last-watered date. The next date is one added to the other. Log early or late and the next date moves with you; the interval itself only changes when you do it twice in the same direction.

Why is the fern on three days and the snake plant on none?

A maidenhair fern in a heated room dries in a day, so it is misted every other day and its tray is checked twice a week. A snake plant wants watering about once a month and less in winter; its next drop is in a fortnight.

Can I move a task?

Yes. Drag it on the grid, or open the plant and change the date. The days after it move too, so the interval holds.
