Visiting the archive

The search room is on the first floor of the chapter house, up a stone stair with no lift. Tell us if that is a difficulty and we will bring the documents down to the vestry.

Rules of the search room

Pencils only

No ink of any kind, including ballpoint. Pencils are on the desk; laptops are welcome, and there is power at every desk but the two by the window.

Bags in the lockers

Coats and bags go in the lockers on the stair. Bring a pound coin for the key; you get it back.

One box at a time

Documents come to the desk one box at a time, on a cushion where they need one. Keep the order they arrive in; the slips between items are there for that.

Photographs, without flash

For your own study, as many as you like. For publication, ask at the desk; the chapter's leave is usually given and takes about a fortnight.
