The cathedral archive, Hollowmere

Eight centuries of the minster, kept in one room.

The archive holds the chapter's charters from 1189, the fabric rolls that paid for every stone, and the chronicle the canons have kept, with gaps, since the tower fell. It is open to anyone with a question and a pencil.

What is kept here

Charters and deeds

Three hundred and twelve, the oldest a grant of the mill at Hollowmere in 1189. Calendared; the calendar is on the desk.

The fabric rolls

Accounts of the building works, year by year, from 1298. Who was paid, for what stone, from which quarry.

The chronicle

Entries by the canons from 1471 to last Tuesday. Two hundred and nineteen so far. Read one.

This month's leaf

The fall of the north-west tower, 1471

The entry that began the chronicle, transcribed with its notes in the margin and the fabric roll that paid for the rebuilding. New leaf on the first of every month.

Questions readers ask

Who may read here?

Anyone. Bring something with your name on it. Children are welcome with an adult; the desks are high and the boxes heavy.

May I photograph documents?

Yes, without flash, for your own study. Publication needs the chapter's leave; ask at the desk and we will write to the Dean for you.

Are the registers online?

The parish registers to 1900 are, through the county record office. Nothing else is, yet. The calendar of charters is being typed and will follow.

i

The search room closes for the Feast of Saint Aldric, 3 to 5 October. Desks booked for those days have been moved to the following week; the hours have the detail.
