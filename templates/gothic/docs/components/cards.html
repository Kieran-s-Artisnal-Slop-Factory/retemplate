Cards

A bordered surface with optional media, a title and text. Vertical by default; horizontal with a class; and one feature card per template with an effect that suits the design. Gothic adds an arched variant and washes the feature card in stained glass.

Arched (Gothic)

Add gothic-arch and the picture is set into the panel as a lancet window; the body text centres under it. Vertical cards only: do not combine it with card-horizontal. A swap to another template drops the class and leaves an ordinary card.

<article class="card gothic-arch">
  <figure class="card-media"><img src="../../assets/card-1.svg" alt="" width="640" height="400"></figure>
  <div class="card-body">
    <h3 class="card-title">The charters</h3>
    <p class="card-text">Three hundred and twelve, the oldest from 1189.</p>
  </div>
</article>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == cards == */.

ClassPurpose
.cardThe surface: border, radius, shadow, flex column
.card-mediaFigure holding an image or an inline SVG
.card-iconAn inline SVG sized and coloured as an icon
.card-bodyPadding and vertical spacing
.card-titleThe heading
.card-textMuted description
.card-actionsButtons at the bottom, pushed down
.card-horizontalMedia beside the body
.card-media-endWith .card-horizontal: media on the right
.card-featureThe template's dynamic card: the stained-glass wash
.gothic-archGothic only: the picture becomes a lancet, the body centres

Notes

  • Cards are <article> elements; use <div> if the content is not self-contained.
  • Lay them out with .grid-2 or .grid-3; they stretch to equal heights.
  • Horizontal cards stack on screens narrower than 30rem.
  • The feature card responds to :hover and :focus-within; it is CSS only. The wash is --gothic-glass and the lead lines are --gothic-lead, both in theme.css; the transition is shortened to nothing under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Card media is clipped to a pointed arch (--gothic-arch). An icon in .card-media sits in a small arched niche of its own instead, so nothing is cut off.
  • Cards have no shadow and square corners. The one shape in the template is the arch, and it appears only where gothic-arch asks for it.
