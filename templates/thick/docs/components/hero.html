Hero

The opening of a page: a heading at the largest size, a paragraph, one or two buttons, and beside them whatever the template leads with. Every template has its own hero form; this page shows Thick's, then two variants that move.

Preview

The hero as the overview uses it. The heading takes --font-size-hero; the paragraph is muted and capped at a readable measure.

<section class="hero">
  <span class="badge thick-sticker">3px lines, 0px blur</span>
  <div class="stack">
    <h1>Press anything. It moves.</h1>
    <p>Every box has a black outline and sits on a solid shadow. Press a button, a thumbnail or a text field and it drops into the page; let go and it pops back. Yellow carries the important things, two pastels carry the rest, and after dark the outlines turn off-white.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>

    </div>
  </div>

</section>

Variants

Text only

The same hero with nothing beside the words. Add hero-text: the grid becomes one column and the stack is capped at a readable measure. Every template carries this variant with the same class.

<section class="hero hero-text">
  <div class="stack">
    <p class="kicker">Words only</p>
    <h1>A hero with nothing beside it.</h1>
    <p>One column, the text measure capped so the lines stay readable, and everything else the hero has: the kicker, the heading at the hero size, the paragraph, the buttons.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
      <a class="btn btn-lg" href="index.html">Browse components</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

Image led

A hero that gives the picture most of the room: the words take one column and the image three, a 25/75 split at desktop widths that stacks on a phone. Add hero-image-half as well for an even 50/50. Every template carries this variant with the same classes.

<section class="hero hero-image">
  <div class="stack">
    <p class="kicker">Picture led</p>
    <h1>The picture takes three quarters.</h1>
    <p>Words in the first column, the picture in the rest.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
    </div>

The drop

The whole box drops onto the page and bounces once as its shadow snaps in, then the heading, the words and the buttons pop up in turn with a little overshoot. The sticker lands last and keeps wobbling. Add thick-drop to the hero. Under prefers-reduced-motion the global rule cuts every animation to nothing, so the hero is simply there.

<section class="hero thick-drop">
  <span class="badge thick-sticker">Just landed</span>
  <div class="stack">
    <h1>Dropped in. Bounced once.</h1>
    <p>The box falls first and its shadow arrives as it lands. Then everything inside pops up one after another. The sticker never quite settles.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
      <a class="btn btn-lg" href="index.html">Browse components</a>
    </div>
  </div>

</section>

The pile-up

Three stickers fly in from off the page and land on the corner of the box one after another, each on top of the last and each at its own tilt. They hit hard, overshoot, and settle, and once they are down they stay. Add thick-pileup to the hero; the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.

<section class="hero thick-pileup">
  <span class="badge thick-sticker">3px lines</span>
  <span class="badge thick-sticker">0px blur</span>
  <span class="badge thick-sticker">100% yellow</span>
  <div class="stack">
    <h1>Stuck on, three deep.</h1>
    <p>Yellow, then pink, then blue, half a second apart. Each lands from a different direction and keeps the tilt it landed with.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>

    </div>
  </div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css: the hero under /* == utilities == */ with the other layout helpers, the variant under /* == hero variants == */.

ClassPurpose
.heroThe opening section: a grid of a text stack and, in most templates, a picture or a piece of flare
.hero .stackThe kicker, heading, paragraph and button row
.hero .rowThe buttons
.hero.thick-dropThe box lands; its stack, pile and sticker follow
.hero.thick-pileupThree .thick-sticker badges land on the corner in turn
.hero.hero-textWords only: one column, capped measure (every template)
.hero.hero-imagePicture led: words one column, picture three; with .hero-image-half an even split (every template)

Notes

  • One <h1> per page: the hero's heading is it. The kicker above it is a <p>, not a heading.
  • Above 48rem the hero is two columns, text then picture; below, it stacks. The variant keeps whatever layout its markup implies.
  • The variant is CSS only. Its animation is declared with both fill, so the page is correct before it starts and after it ends, and the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.
  • Another template ignores thick-drop and shows its own hero; any flare markup inside sits in the template's flare wrapper so a swap can drop it.
