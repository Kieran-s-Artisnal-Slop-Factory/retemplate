About

Three people, one risograph, forty copies at a time

Paper Cuts started in 2023 because Ines kept explaining the same program to the same friends and decided to print it once instead. Tomasz draws, Priya keeps the books and writes the ones about spreadsheets. The shop is a room in an old print works and is open on Saturdays.

Ines

Writes most of the issues and runs the machine. Has opinions about floppy disks and will tell you them.

Tomasz

Draws every frame of every fold-out by hand and then scans them. Wrote issue 2, which was one word long on the cover.

Priya

Keeps the shop solvent and the subscriber list in a spreadsheet, which is also the subject of issue 8.

Writing one

What we look for

One program, tool or habit, and the one thing about it you cannot stop telling people. Not a review, not a tutorial. Twelve pages at our size is about 2,500 words and six drawings, or fewer words and more drawings.

What you get

Ten copies, a third of what the issue makes after printing and post, and your name on the cover. Tomasz draws unless you would rather.

How to send it

Email a paragraph, not a draft, to hello@example.com. We answer everything, usually within two weeks, always before the next issue goes to print.
