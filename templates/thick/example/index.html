Small zines about big software.

Paper Cuts is a shop with one shelf. Every issue is twelve pages on one program, tool or habit, written by the person who uses it most, printed on a risograph and stapled at the kitchen table. Forty copies of each. When they are gone, they are gone.

Questions people email us

Do you post outside the country?

Yes, anywhere the post goes. It costs 3.00 more and takes one to three weeks. Customs forms say "printed matter", because it is.

Can I get a PDF instead?

No. Every issue is laid out for paper and half of them fold out. If an issue sells out we reprint it the following season and say so on the issues page.

Can I write one?

Maybe. Email us the program and the one thing about it you cannot stop telling people. Twelve pages is shorter than it sounds. The about page says what we look for.
