Flare

Flare is everything a template adds beyond the contract. Termy adds the window, a terminal frame with a transcript inside, and the CRT, an overlay on the whole page that is one class and two tokens. The prompts, hashes, brackets and cursor need no markup: they are generated content on the contract's own classes.

The window

A frame with three dots in its title bar and a <pre class="termy-prompt"> transcript. <b> is a prompt, termy-ok, termy-err and termy-dim colour a line. The overview and the example home use one as the hero's picture.

<div class="termy-flare">
  <div class="termy-window">
    <div class="termy-titlebar"><span></span><span></span><span></span>shelf — 80×24</div>
<pre class="termy-prompt"><b>$</b> shelf sync
<span class="termy-ok">✓</span> 3 notes pushed  <span class="termy-dim">0.2s</span>
<b>$</b> shelf rm 0009
<span class="termy-err">✗</span> refusing: note is pinned</pre>
  </div>
</div>

The CRT

With termy-crt on <body> the page gets scanlines and a vignette fixed over it, a phosphor glow on the text in the green scheme, and a flicker once every five seconds. --termy-crt-strength (0 to 1) sets how strong the overlay is and --termy-crt-flicker: 0 stops the flicker. Every page in this folder ships with it on at 0.5.

<div class="termy-flare">
  <p>Turn the screen down: <code>&lt;body class="termy-crt" style="--termy-crt-strength: 0.2"&gt;</code>. Turn it off: drop the class.</p>
</div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == termy == */.

ClassPurpose
.termy-flareThe wrapper for flare that is markup; strip it when swapping templates
.termy-window, .termy-titlebarThe frame and its title bar with three dots
pre.termy-promptThe transcript; b is a prompt
.termy-ok, .termy-err, .termy-dimA line in success, danger or muted
body.termy-crtThe overlay, the glow and the flicker
--termy-crt-strength, --termy-crt-flickerThe two knobs, settable inline or in theme.css

Notes

  • The overlay is pointer-events: none and sits above everything, including dialogs. Lower the strength if a dialog needs to read cleanly.
  • The flicker is a fixed layer that flashes at 5% for a frame; reduced motion stops it and the cursor.
  • Flare must still use tokens: no colour literals in global.css. The scanlines, vignette, glows and flash are --termy-* tokens in theme.css.
