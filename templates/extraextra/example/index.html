The room

Brick, steel and a two-metre stage.

The Kiln was a pottery until 1988 and the walls still hold the heat. Four hundred standing, a balcony for sixty, a PA that was too big for the room on purpose, and a bar along the back wall that serves until the last band finishes.

Read the house rules

House rules

Short, because the security team will tell you the rest.

Ages

Fourteen and over for every show unless the line-up says otherwise. Under-eighteens stand in the balcony after ten. Bring photo ID if you look under twenty-five; the bar will ask.

Re-entry

None after ten o'clock. There is a smoking yard through the door by the merch table, so you do not need to leave.

Bags, cameras, earplugs

Small bags only; there is no cloakroom. Phones are fine, cameras with a detachable lens are not. Free earplugs are at the bar and you should take them: the room is loud even by our standards.

Access

Level entry from Kiln Yard, an accessible toilet on the ground floor and a raised viewing platform stage left. Email us before the show and we will keep a space on it.
