Questions at the door

Can I get a refund?

If the show is cancelled or moved, yes, in full, without asking. If you cannot come, put the ticket on the returns list from the line-up page and we sell it on for you at face value.

Do I need to print anything?

No. Show the email on your phone, or tell us the name on the door list. If your phone dies in the queue, the name is enough.

What time should I arrive?

Doors are at seven and the first band walks on at eight. If you want the barrier, be in the queue by half past six. If you only want the headliner, half past nine is fine and the queue is gone.

Is there a box office?

A hatch on Kiln Yard, open from six on show nights only. Cash or card, same prices as here, as long as the show has not sold out.
