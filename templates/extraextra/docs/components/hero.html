Component

Hero

The opening of a page: a heading at the largest size, a paragraph, one or two buttons, and beside them whatever the template leads with. Every template has its own hero form; this page shows ExtraExtra's, then a second variant that moves.

Preview

The hero as the overview uses it. The heading takes --font-size-hero; the paragraph is muted and capped at a readable measure.

<section class="hero">
  <div class="stack">
    <p class="kicker">Full width. Two colours. One typeface.</p>
    <h1>Loud on purpose.</h1>
    <p>ExtraExtra is a poster wall: paper and black type by day, black and orange by night, hard rules, no corners, no shadows, and a condensed face set in capitals as large as the page allows. The markup is the same as every other retemplate template. Only the stylesheets and the art shout.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
      <a class="btn btn-lg" href="index.html">Browse components</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

Variants

The slap

Each word of the headline is a poster slapped onto the wall: the first from the left, the second from the right, the third from the left again, each landing a beat after the last with a little overshoot and a tilt. A round sticker spins on to finish. Add extraextra-slap to the hero. Under prefers-reduced-motion the global rule cuts every animation to nothing, so the hero is simply there.

<section class="hero extraextra-slap">
  <span class="badge extraextra-sticker">Tonight</span>
  <div class="stack">
    <p class="kicker">Three posters. One wall.</p>
    <h1><span>Slapped</span> <span>on</span> <span>crooked.</span></h1>
    <p>Orange, ink, orange. Each word is its own poster and lands from its own side, a quarter second apart. The sticker spins in last. Under reduced motion they are simply up.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
      <a class="btn btn-lg" href="index.html">Browse components</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css: the hero under /* == utilities == */ with the other layout helpers, the variant under /* == hero variants == */.

ClassPurpose
.heroThe opening section: a grid of a text stack and, in most templates, a picture or a piece of flare
.hero .stackThe kicker, heading, paragraph and button row
.hero .rowThe buttons
.hero.extraextra-slapThe wall; each h1 span is a poster
.extraextra-slap h1 > spanA poster; odd ones orange from the left, the second ink from the right
.badge.extraextra-stickerThe round sticker that spins on last

Notes

  • One <h1> per page: the hero's heading is it. The kicker above it is a <p>, not a heading.
  • Above 48rem the hero is two columns, text then picture; below, it stacks. The variant keeps whatever layout its markup implies.
  • The variant is CSS only. Its animation is declared with both fill, so the page is correct before it starts and after it ends, and the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.
  • Another template ignores extraextra-slap and shows its own hero; any flare markup inside sits in the template's flare wrapper so a swap can drop it.
