Flare

Flare is everything a template adds beyond the contract. ExtraExtra adds three pieces of poster-wall furniture: an orange band, an ink band and the ticker. Four more are variants of contract components and live on their own pages: the poster card, the shout button, the set-times table and the strip banner. The ten-pixel bar above the navbar, the orange scrim behind a dialog and the two-colour feature card need no class at all.

Orange band

Put a section in extraextra-band and it runs edge to edge in the one bright orange with black type, ruled top and bottom. The heading grows to band size, the paragraph is capped at a readable measure, and buttons redraw in black on orange. Bands cancel the page gutter with a negative margin, so put them directly inside main.page; in the sidebar layout they stop at the index. A fifty-fifty inside a band drops its own box. This is the only place the bright orange meets type, and the type is always black: the orange fails 4.5:1 against paper as a text colour, which is why links use the burnt orange instead.

<section class="extraextra-band">
  <p class="kicker">Saturday 4 October</p>
  <h2>Two stages. One night.</h2>
  <p>Kettle Drum Club headline the main room while the loft runs an all-night DJ set. One ticket covers both.</p>
  <a class="btn btn-primary" href="#">Buy tickets</a>
</section>

Ink band

extraextra-band-ink is the page in reverse: ink ground, paper type, and an orange kicker mark. In the dark scheme the ink is paper, so the band becomes the one pale strip on a black page. Same rules as the orange band: directly inside main.page, one heading, a short paragraph, at most one button.

<section class="extraextra-band-ink">
  <p class="kicker">Hire the room</p>
  <h2>Four hundred people, no seats.</h2>
  <p>The Kiln is available on Mondays and Tuesdays for launches, screenings and private shows. The PA and the engineer come with it.</p>
  <a class="btn btn-primary" href="#">Ask about a date</a>
</section>

The ticker

The signature. An ink strip the width of the page carrying short mono facts from right to left, with an orange square between each. The markup is two copies of the same <p>, the second aria-hidden, so the loop has no gap; each <span> is one fact. It pauses while hovered or focused, and stops under prefers-reduced-motion. One loop takes --extraextra-ticker-speed, 45 seconds; shorten it for fewer facts. The wrapper is extraextra-flare, so a template swap can remove the whole thing. The overview runs it under the hero; a venue runs this week's dates.

<div class="extraextra-flare">
  <div class="extraextra-ticker">
    <p><span>Fri 3 Oct: Slow Furnace</span><span>Sat 4 Oct: Kettle Drum Club</span><span>Thu 9 Oct: Ida Vane</span><span>Sat 11 Oct: The Stokers, sold out</span><span>Doors 7, bands 8</span></p>
    <p aria-hidden="true"><span>Fri 3 Oct: Slow Furnace</span><span>Sat 4 Oct: Kettle Drum Club</span><span>Thu 9 Oct: Ida Vane</span><span>Sat 11 Oct: The Stokers, sold out</span><span>Doors 7, bands 8</span></p>
  </div>
</div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == flare == */, the variants included: they sit at the end of that section, after the bands and the ticker.

ClassPurpose
.extraextra-flareThe wrapper for flare that is markup; strip it when swapping templates
.extraextra-bandFull-bleed orange section, black type, ruled top and bottom
.extraextra-band-inkFull-bleed section in the type colour with the page colour as type
.extraextra-tickerThe moving ink strip; two <p> of <span> facts
.card.extraextra-posterA card with no box and an ink bar on top (cards)
.btn.extraextra-shoutA button in display capitals at heading size (buttons)
.table-wrap.extraextra-timesThe set-times board (table)
.banner.extraextra-stripA full-bleed ink notice with an orange label (banner)

Notes

  • Every colour here is a token: --extraextra-orange and --extraextra-on-orange for the orange band, the semantic --text-color and --bg-color for the ink band and the ticker, so the ink band flips with the scheme and the orange band does not. Nothing in global.css names a colour.
  • The ticker is the only thing in the template that moves on its own. It pauses on hover and focus, and the global prefers-reduced-motion rule sets its animation to none, so the first copy of the facts sits still and the hidden copy stays out of view.
  • The facts in the ticker are content and are read once by assistive technology; the second <p> is aria-hidden so they are not read twice. Keep each fact short enough to read in passing.
  • Bands and the strip cancel the gutter with margin-inline: calc(-1 * var(--extraextra-gutter)). Inside a docs example, or any other padded box, they sit flush instead; put them where the gutter is if they are to reach the edge.
  • Focus rings inside a band are drawn in black on orange and in orange on ink, so they are visible on both.
