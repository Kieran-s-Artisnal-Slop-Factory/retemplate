Hero

The opening of a page: a heading at the largest size, a paragraph, one or two buttons, and beside them whatever the template leads with. Every template has its own hero form; this page shows Neon's, then two variants that move.

Preview

The hero as the overview uses it. The heading takes --font-size-hero; the paragraph is muted and capped at a readable measure.

<section class="hero neon-stage">
  <div class="stack">
    <h1><span class="neon-text neon-strike">Open all night.</span></h1>
    <p>Neon is the dark template twice. Light is zinc, dark is black, and nothing on these pages is ever white. Glow is state: what is lit is on.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

Variants

Text only

The same hero with nothing beside the words. Add hero-text: the grid becomes one column and the stack is capped at a readable measure. Every template carries this variant with the same class.

<section class="hero hero-text">
  <div class="stack">
    <p class="kicker">Words only</p>
    <h1>A hero with nothing beside it.</h1>
    <p>One column, the text measure capped so the lines stay readable, and everything else the hero has: the kicker, the heading at the hero size, the paragraph, the buttons.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
      <a class="btn btn-lg" href="index.html">Browse components</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

Image led

A hero that gives the picture most of the room: the words take one column and the image three, a 25/75 split at desktop widths that stacks on a phone. Add hero-image-half as well for an even 50/50. Every template carries this variant with the same classes.

<section class="hero hero-image">
  <div class="stack">
    <p class="kicker">Picture led</p>
    <h1>The picture takes three quarters.</h1>
    <p>Words in the first column, the picture in the rest.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
    </div>
  </div>
  <img src="../../assets/hero.svg" alt="" width="1200" height="750">
</section>

The chase

A run of lights chases around the edge of the stage: a dashed cyan line on an inline SVG, its dashes moving along the outline like bulbs around a sign. The headline lights up behind it. Add neon-chase to the hero. Under prefers-reduced-motion the global rule cuts every animation to nothing, so the hero is simply there.

<section class="hero neon-stage neon-chase">
  <svg class="neon-chase-lights" aria-hidden="true"><rect pathLength="100"/></svg>
  <div class="stack">
    <h1><span class="neon-text">Lights, chasing.</span></h1>
    <p>The bulbs around the sign are one dashed stroke with its offset on a loop. Cyan for the frame, rose for what you can press.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

The switch-on

The sign comes on one word at a time. Each word starts dark, stutters twice as the gas catches, and holds lit, and the next word starts a beat later. It plays once; after that the sign is simply on. Add neon-switch-on to the hero; the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.

<section class="hero neon-stage neon-switch-on">
  <div class="stack">
    <h1><span class="neon-text">Open.</span> <span class="neon-text">All.</span> <span class="neon-text">Night.</span></h1>
    <p>Three tubes, three switches, half a second apart. The stutter is four stepped frames; the hold is the rest of the page.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css: the hero under /* == utilities == */ with the other layout helpers, the variant under /* == hero variants == */.

ClassPurpose
.heroThe opening section: a grid of a text stack and, in most templates, a picture or a piece of flare
.hero .stackThe kicker, heading, paragraph and button row
.hero .rowThe buttons
.hero.neon-chaseThe stage with the lights around it
.neon-chase-lightsThe inline SVG; its rect is one dashed stroke on a loop
.hero.neon-stage.neon-switch-onEach .neon-text word in the heading catches in turn
.hero.hero-textWords only: one column, capped measure (every template)
.hero.hero-imagePicture led: words one column, picture three; with .hero-image-half an even split (every template)

Notes

  • One <h1> per page: the hero's heading is it. The kicker above it is a <p>, not a heading.
  • Above 48rem the hero is two columns, text then picture; below, it stacks. The variant keeps whatever layout its markup implies.
  • The variant is CSS only. Its animation is declared with both fill, so the page is correct before it starts and after it ends, and the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.
  • Another template ignores neon-chase and shows its own hero; any flare markup inside sits in the template's flare wrapper so a swap can drop it.
