Component

Flare

Flare is what a template adds beyond the contract. Neon adds light, under one rule: glow is state. The signature is the sign, a headline set as tube lettering that strikes once when the page opens. Around it are a lit strip, a dated rail, a pill label and glow as a class. Only the rail is markup; the rest are classes on elements you already have. The component variants that follow the same rule live on their own pages: the panel, the state light, the status board and the outline button.

The sign

.neon-text turns a run of words into a tube: the accent colour with a layered text-shadow, so it works at any size. .neon-text-cyan is the cooler tube. Add .neon-strike and the tube strikes once, about two seconds of stutter, then holds; under prefers-reduced-motion it is simply on. Use the strike on one headline per page, not on labels.

<h2><span class="neon-text neon-strike">Open all night.</span> <span class="neon-text-cyan">Closed Sundays.</span></h2>

The stage

The hero without a picture. .neon-stage on .hero drops the two-column grid, sets the headline very large in Space Grotesk, uppercase and tight, and closes with a hairline. One paragraph and one call under it. The overview and every example page open this way; the headline is the page's state in two or three words.

<section class="hero neon-stage">
  <div class="stack">
    <h1><span class="neon-text neon-strike">Three plans.</span></h1>
    <p>One paragraph under the sign, and one call.</p>
    <div class="row"><a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="#variants">Start free</a></div>
  </div>
</section>

The marquee

A lit heading strip: small tracked caps between two hairlines, items separated by cyan lamps. It carries the facts a page wants read first, a spec line or a status line, and sits at the top of main before the stage. A <ul> of short items; the first is usually <strong>.

<ul class="neon-marquee">
  <li><strong>Relay</strong></li>
  <li>Nine regions</li>
  <li>Checks every thirty seconds</li>
  <li><a href="#variants">Subscribe</a></li>
</ul>

The rail

A dated list. A tube runs down the left; each entry hangs a date beside a lamp and puts its content to the right. .neon-rail-current lights the lamp of the entry that is now. Entries are plain <li>s: a <time>, a heading (with a version light in a .row if you like), paragraphs, tags. The example changelog is one.

<ol class="neon-rail">
  <li class="neon-rail-current">
    <time datetime="2026-09-01">1 Sep 2026</time>
    <div class="row"><span class="badge neon-light neon-light-cyan">2.4</span><h3>Incident templates</h3></div>
    <p>The current entry. Its lamp is lit.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <time datetime="2026-08-18">18 Aug 2026</time>
    <div class="row"><span class="badge neon-light">2.3</span><h3>Heartbeat monitors</h3></div>
    <p>An earlier entry. Its lamp is dark.</p>
  </li>
</ol>

The pill

A lit pill label with a steady lamp. Use it where a plainer template would use a kicker and the label is a state: "Recommended" on a plan, "Live" on a page. It can be a <span> or a link. .neon-sign-cyan for information.

<p class="row"><span class="neon-sign">Recommended</span> <a class="neon-sign neon-sign-cyan" href="#variants">Read the notes</a></p>

Glow on a box

The glow the lit panel and the dialog use, as a class for any block. Rose by default, cyan with the suffix. It is a box-shadow, so it sits under the element and costs nothing.

<div class="grid-2">
  <article class="card neon-glow"><div class="card-body"><h3 class="card-title">Rose</h3><p class="card-text"><code>.neon-glow</code> on an ordinary card.</p></div></article>
  <article class="card neon-glow-cyan"><div class="card-body"><h3 class="card-title">Cyan</h3><p class="card-text"><code>.neon-glow-cyan</code> on another.</p></div></article>
</div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == flare == */, except .neon-stage, which sits with .hero under /* == utilities == */.

ClassPurpose
.neon-text, .neon-text-cyanTube lettering: colour plus layered text-shadow
.neon-strikeWith .neon-text: strikes once on load, then holds
.neon-stageOn .hero: no picture, one column, the headline as the sign
.neon-marqueeA lit heading strip of short caps items
.neon-rail, .neon-rail-currentA dated list on a tube; the current entry's lamp is lit
.neon-sign, .neon-sign-cyanA lit pill label with a lamp
.neon-glow, .neon-glow-cyanThe glow shadow on any block
.neon-panel, .neon-light, .neon-status, .neon-outlineComponent variants; see cards, badge, table, buttons

Notes

  • Every glow is built from two tokens in theme.css, --neon-rose and --neon-cyan, the two accents at half alpha. Change those two and every glow follows. Nothing in global.css names a colour.
  • Two things move: the strike, once, and the feature card's border sweep on hover. Both stop under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce; the light stays on and holds still. There is no ambient animation.
  • Light one thing per view. A page with three lit panels and a lit headline has no state left to show.
  • The feature card is not flare; it is the contract's .card-feature. Its sweep is Neon's: a conic gradient shown through the card's own hairline, turned by a registered @property angle. See Cards.
  • A template swap strips every .neon-* class. The rail and the marquee then read as an ordinary list; the stage as an ordinary hero.
