Component

Flare

Flare is everything a template adds beyond the contract. Neon adds light: a haze behind the page, glow you can put on text or on a box, and a lit sign for the one label a page wants to shout. Only the haze is markup; the rest are classes.

The haze

Two long blurred tubes — one rose, one cyan — fixed behind the whole page, breathing slowly. Put the block right after <body>; it is aria-hidden and never intercepts a click. It is the only flare that needs a .neon-flare wrapper. (The preview below is inert because it sits inside the page rather than behind it.)

<div class="neon-flare neon-haze" aria-hidden="true">
  <div class="neon-tube"></div>
  <div class="neon-tube"></div>
</div>

Lit text

Wrap the words that matter. .neon-text is rose, .neon-text-cyan is cyan; both are a colour and a text-shadow, nothing more, so they work on any heading size.

<h2>Ship on <span class="neon-text">Friday</span>, sleep on <span class="neon-text-cyan">Saturday</span>.</h2>

The sign

A pill with a lit border, a dot that flickers once every few seconds, and uppercase display type. Use it where a plainer template would use a kicker: above a hero heading, on a pricing tier, on a changelog entry. It can be a <span> or a link.

<p class="row"><span class="neon-sign">New this week</span> <a class="neon-sign neon-sign-cyan" href="#variants">Read the notes</a></p>

Glow on a box

The same glow the hero image and the feature card use, as a class for any block. Rose by default, cyan with the suffix. It is a box-shadow, so it sits under the element and costs nothing on hover.

<div class="grid-2">
  <article class="card neon-glow"><div class="card-body"><h3 class="card-title">Rose</h3><p class="card-text"><code>.neon-glow</code> on an ordinary card.</p></div></article>
  <article class="card neon-glow-cyan"><div class="card-body"><h3 class="card-title">Cyan</h3><p class="card-text"><code>.neon-glow-cyan</code> on another.</p></div></article>
</div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == flare == */.

ClassPurpose
.neon-hazeFixed, full-viewport, behind everything; holds the two tubes
.neon-tubeA blurred bar; the first child is rose, the second cyan, on a slow breathe
.neon-text, .neon-text-cyanLit words: colour plus text-shadow
.neon-sign, .neon-sign-cyanA lit pill label with a flickering dot
.neon-glow, .neon-glow-cyanThe template's glow shadow on any block
.neon-flareThe wrapper for flare that is markup (only the haze needs it)

Notes

  • Every glow is built from two tokens in theme.css, --neon-rose and --neon-cyan, which are the two accents at half alpha. Change those two and every glow on the site follows. Nothing in global.css names a colour.
  • The haze's breathing, the sign's flicker and the feature card's border sweep all stop under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce; the light stays on, it just holds still.
  • The feature card is not flare — it is the contract's .card-feature — but its sweep is Neon's: a conic gradient shown through the card's own border and turned by a registered @property angle. See Cards.
  • The haze is two large blurred elements. It is cheap, but on very long pages with many fixed layers you can drop it and keep the static haze that body already carries.
