Open all night.

Neon is the dark template. The light scheme lifts the ground to grey and stops there; nothing on these pages is ever white. Glow is state: what is lit is on.

Get started

Lit is on. Dark is off.

Every control reads the same way. A filled or lit tube is active; a hairline is idle. The outline button is Neon's quiet call, the switch lights when checked, and a badge with a lamp in front of it is a state light: green up, amber degraded, rose down, grey off.

Light scheme

Less dark. Never white.

Light lifts the page from #18181b to #3a3a42 and steps the surfaces up from there. Text stays off-white; links move to a lighter rose so both halves clear 4.5:1. Native controls, dialogs and open menus are pinned to the dark scheme, so the browser never paints a white picker over a dark page.

Service notes

Why is the light scheme still dark?

Because a neon sign on a white wall is a sticker. The light half's page colour is #3a3a42 and the surfaces step up from there. Text stays off-white in both halves; links take a lighter rose so they clear 4.5:1 on the lifted ground.

Why do form fields stay black in light mode?

Both halves are dark, so the template pins native controls, dialogs and open menus to the dark scheme. The browser never paints a white dropdown or date picker. Inside those elements the palette resolves to its dark half, so a field is always a black well.

How much JavaScript is there?

About forty-five lines for the scheme toggle, and a one-line onclick on anything that opens a dialog. The glow, the sweep and the strike are CSS.

Eight signs

Click one. The lightbox is a <dialog> with a cyan glow; the arrows are links and Esc closes it.

Signals

Banners carry a message with its colour on the edge. Cyan is information, so it never reads as an error; rose is kept for actions and for danger. The dialog is native: it traps focus, dims the page and closes on Esc.

Words

Prose is what most pages are made of, so .prose styles every construct a markdown renderer produces: headings in Space Grotesk, body in Inter, inline code in cyan, quotes with a lit edge. The blog post runs the full test document through it; defaults shows every bare element.

A dark theme is not a light theme with the values swapped. It is a room with the lights off and a few of them on.
