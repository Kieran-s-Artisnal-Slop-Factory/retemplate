Incident templates

Write the human sentence once and reuse it: "Elevated errors in EU-West", "Planned maintenance", "Third-party outage". Heartbeat monitors

For the things that call you: cron jobs, queue workers, nightly backups. Relay gives each one a URL; if the ping does not arrive inside the window, the monitor goes amber, then red. Grace periods are per monitor.

Incidents we learned from

The status page for Relay itself is run by someone else, but the write-ups are ours. Open one.

22 August — subscriber emails delayed by 40 minutes

A queue worker in the notification service was restarted with an old config and pointed at a retired mail relay. Checks passed because the relay accepted connections; it just never delivered. We now heartbeat the mail path end to end, with a mailbox that expects a message every five minutes.

×

Impact: 1,240 incident emails delivered late. No status pages were affected.

9 August — false "degraded" on 31 monitors in Sydney

An upstream DNS resolver in the Sydney region began returning SERVFAIL for a fraction of lookups. A single region cannot trigger an incident, so nothing went red, but customers saw Sydney listed as failing for 22 minutes. We now try a second resolver before recording a DNS failure.

!

Impact: cosmetic. No incident was opened, no email was sent.

27 July — planned maintenance, 15 minutes

Database version upgrade. Announced a week ahead, finished in eleven minutes, checks continued from a read replica throughout. Included here because a maintenance that goes to plan is still worth writing down.

✓

Impact: none. The dashboard was read-only for eleven minutes.
