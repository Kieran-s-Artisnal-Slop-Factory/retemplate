Hours and the way here

Asked at the potting bench

Will it grow in my garden?

Tell us what your soil does after rain (puddles, drains, cracks) and whether the spot sees the sun at noon. Those two facts settle most of it. Every sheet in the field guide carries the same two lines.

Do you deliver?

Within the valley, on Tuesdays, by van. Further than that we post seed and bare-root trees in winter, but not pots.

What if it dies?

Bring the label back within a year and we replace it, and talk about why. Usually it is the drainage.
