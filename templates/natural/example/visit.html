Come and see

Visit the nursery

We are at the end of a farm track two miles south of Ashcombe. The gate is open when the sign is out. Wear boots; the beds are clay and the paths are grass.

Getting here

By car

Leave Ashcombe on the Fallow road and take the track after the second bridge. Park on the hardstanding by the barn.

By bike

The valley cycleway passes the bottom of the track. There is a rack under the walnut tree and a pump in the glasshouse.

On foot

Forty minutes from Ashcombe church along the river path. Muddy after rain, which is most of the year.

Ask about a plant, or book a bench

Tell us what you have in mind and we will write back within the week. On a Saturday you can also book half an hour at the potting bench with whoever grew the plant.

While you are here

Can I bring pots back?

Please. Terracotta goes back on the shelf; plastic goes to the grower who supplied it. Leave them by the barn door.

Is the nursery accessible?

The glasshouse, the tea table and the first two beds are on hard paths and level. The lower beds are grass on a slope; ask and we will bring plants up to you.

Can I walk the wood?

Yes, along the marked path. It is where most of the ferns in the field guide were first collected, and in April it is full of anemones.
