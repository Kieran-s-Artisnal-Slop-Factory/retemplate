Component

Accordion

A disclosure with a title and a body. Alone it is a <details> with a class; several with a shared name become an exclusive group.

Preview

<details class="accordion">
  <summary>How does it open?</summary>
  <div class="accordion-body"><p>It is a native <code>&lt;details&gt;</code> element. The browser handles open and closed, and the summary is keyboard-focusable on its own.</p></div>
</details>

Variants

Exclusive group

Wrap accordions in .accordion-group and give them the same name. The browser keeps at most one open.

<div class="accordion-group">
  <details class="accordion" name="group-a" open>
    <summary>First</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body"><p>Open by default.</p></div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion" name="group-a">
    <summary>Second</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body"><p>Opening this closes the first.</p></div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion" name="group-a">
    <summary>Third</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body"><p>And this closes whichever was open.</p></div>
  </details>
</div>

Key

Natural's own variant. Add natural-key to an exclusive group and it is set as a dichotomous key, the form a field guide uses to name a plant: the leads come in pairs, numbered 1a, 1b, 2a, 2b by a CSS counter, and each body says what the lead is or where it sends you. Put the leads in order; the counter reads the position, so a stray odd accordion will throw the numbering off.

<div class="accordion-group natural-key">
  <details class="accordion" name="key-docs" open>
    <summary>Frond undivided, a single glossy strap</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body">
      <p class="natural-binomial">Asplenium scolopendrium <span class="natural-auth">L.</span></p>
      <p>Hart's-tongue fern. Walls, well mouths, the foot of north-facing steps.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion" name="key-docs">
    <summary>Frond divided into leaflets</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body"><p>Go to 2.</p></div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion" name="key-docs">
    <summary>Leaflets soft, a thumb at the base and a bristle at the tip</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body">
      <p class="natural-binomial">Polystichum setiferum <span class="natural-auth">(Forssk.) T. Moore ex Woyn.</span></p>
      <p>Soft shield fern. Hedge banks and the shady side of the wood.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion" name="key-docs">
    <summary>Leaflets blunt, the frond stiff in a shuttlecock</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body">
      <p class="natural-binomial">Dryopteris filix-mas <span class="natural-auth">(L.) Schott</span></p>
      <p>Male fern. Almost anywhere that is not baked.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == accordion == */.

ClassPurpose
.accordionThe details element: border, background, padding
summaryThe clickable title; the marker is a plus that rotates
.accordion-bodyPadding around the content
.accordion-groupJoins several into one bordered stack; hosts the exclusive name
.natural-keyNatural's variant on .accordion-group: couplet numbers before each summary, bodies indented to match (global.css § flare)

Notes

  • No script. <details name> is what makes a group exclusive.
  • Keyboard: Tab to the summary, Enter or Space to toggle.
  • Put open on one accordion to start it open.
  • The key's numbers are ::before generated content, so they are not part of the summary's text. Readers hear the lead itself, which is what matters.
