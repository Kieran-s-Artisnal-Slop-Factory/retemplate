Cards

A bordered surface with optional media, a title and text. Vertical by default; horizontal with a class; and one feature card per template with an effect that suits the design.

Specimen card

Natural's own variant. Add natural-specimen and the picture is mounted on the sheet with a margin round it and the body reads as a herbarium label: a common name in Fraunces, the binomial in italic with its authority in roman, and a <dl class="natural-data"> of where it came from and what it wants. The list keeps the data as data. A .card-actions row still works at the bottom.

<article class="card natural-specimen">
  <figure class="card-media"><img src="../../assets/card-3.svg" alt="" width="640" height="400"></figure>
  <div class="card-body">
    <p class="kicker">No. 12</p>
    <h3 class="card-title">Hart's-tongue fern</h3>
    <p class="natural-binomial">Asplenium scolopendrium <span class="natural-auth">L.</span></p>
    <dl class="natural-data">

      <dt>Habit</dt><dd>Evergreen fern, 45 cm</dd>
      <dt>Soil</dt><dd>Lime, never dry</dd>
    </dl>
  </div>
</article>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == cards == */.

ClassPurpose
.cardThe surface: border, radius, shadow, flex column
.card-mediaFigure holding an image or an inline SVG
.card-iconAn inline SVG sized and coloured as an icon
.card-bodyPadding and vertical spacing
.card-titleThe heading
.card-textMuted description
.card-actionsButtons at the bottom, pushed down
.card-horizontalMedia beside the body
.card-media-endWith .card-horizontal: media on the right
.card-featureThe template's dynamic card
.natural-specimenNatural's variant: media mounted with a margin, body set as a label (global.css § flare)
.natural-binomialA Latin name in Fraunces italic; .natural-auth inside it is the authority, roman and small
.natural-dataA two-column <dl>: small-caps field, roman value

Notes

  • Cards are <article> elements; use <div> if the content is not self-contained.
  • Lay them out with .grid-2 or .grid-3; they stretch to equal heights.
  • Horizontal cards stack on screens narrower than 30rem.
  • The feature card responds to :hover and :focus-within; it is CSS only. The tape strips are pseudo-elements on .card-media and the tag is ::after on the card, so the markup is the same as every other card.
  • The tag reads Specimen by default. Rename it per card with style="--natural-specimen-label: 'Fern'", or change the token in theme.css.
  • The specimen card is a variant of the ordinary card, not of the feature card; the two can sit in one grid. Its label type (.natural-binomial, .natural-data) is the same as the plate's on the flare page.
