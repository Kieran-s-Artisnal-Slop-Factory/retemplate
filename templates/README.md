# Templates

One folder per template. Every folder follows the
[template contract](../docs/dev/template-contract.md): the same pages, the
same components, the same class names; only the design differs. Take a
folder and keep its `css/`, `js/theme.js`, `fonts/` and `assets/`; the rest
is the showcase.

| Template | Folder | One line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain | [`templates/plain/`](plain/index.html) | The starting point: every component in a quiet style, on system fonts. |
| Neumorphic | [`templates/neumorphic/`](neumorphic/index.html) | Soft UI: surfaces pushed out of one colour by paired shadows; controls raise, inputs sink, buttons press. Cool grey by day, charcoal by night. |
| Brutal News | [`templates/brutal-news/`](brutal-news/index.html) | A brutalist newspaper: thick black rules, hard offset shadows, acid yellow and cobalt against white paper and hot red. Headlines in Archivo Black, data in JetBrains Mono. |
| Glassy | [`templates/glassy/`](glassy/index.html) | Frosted glass panels over a soft gradient, blurred colour orbs behind them, and four layers of slowly moving waves. Pill buttons, big radii, system fonts. |
| Neon | [`templates/neon/`](neon/index.html) | Black zinc, hairline borders and two neon tubes: rose for actions, cyan for information, glowing rather than filling. Both schemes are dark: light is zinc, dark is true black. Space Grotesk and Inter. |
| Natural | [`templates/natural/`](natural/index.html) | Cream paper, hairline rules, rust and moss, Fraunces headings; deep-brown soil texture with cream and light green after dark. Feature card is a pressed specimen with a lifting paper tag. |
| Detailed | [`templates/detailed/`](detailed/index.html) | Art deco: cream, black ink and bronze by day, navy and gold by night; sunbursts, stepped corners, chevron rules and a gold-leaf sheen. Limelight, Josefin Sans and Jost. Example: a grand hotel. |
| Gothic | [`templates/gothic/`](gothic/index.html) | Pale stone, iron ink and crimson with UnifrakturMaguntia on h1 and h2 only; near-black with candle gold after dark. One pointed arch on pictures, gallery and cards, tracery rules as masks, an illuminated initial. Feature card fills with stained glass on hover. |
| Allegory | [`templates/allegory/`](allegory/index.html) | Two faces of one site: ivory, antique gold and wings by day, off-black, red and horns by night; display type white on gold or black on red. Cormorant Garamond and Inter. Two classes pin an element to one face; the feature card turns over to the other. |
| ExtraExtra | [`templates/extraextra/`](extraextra/index.html) | A poster wall: warm paper and near-black type, one bright orange for full-bleed bands that only carry black type, 2px rules, no page width, no corners, no shadows. Anton in capitals, Barlow body, IBM Plex Mono data; a ticker of facts under the hero. Dark is the same wall in reverse. Example: The Kiln, a music venue. |
| Breathe | [`templates/breathe/`](breathe/index.html) | White-green air, mint panels, a leaf cut on pictures, Manrope with one Instrument Serif italic word; deep forest green after dark. A frond sways in the hero; a stem turns cards into a schedule. Example: Windowsill, a plant-care app. |
| Modern Frosty | [`templates/modern-frosty/`](modern-frosty/index.html) | Frozen solid: a sky of ice fixed behind the page and every pane on it a sheet of frost, blurred at 18px with a rime of light on its top edge. The dock is a frosted pill navbar floating clear of the page; the ink slab is the page turned over. Heavy tight headlines in Geist, a line of Newsreader italic, one deep blue and one warm ember. Example: Sheaf, a notes app. |
| Thick | [`templates/thick/`](thick/index.html) | Neobrutalism that smiles: 3px outlines, solid offset shadows that collapse on press, one bright yellow and two pastels, 8px corners, Rubik at 900. Warm paper by day, near-black with off-white outlines by night. Example: Paper Cuts, a zine shop. |
| Cattle | [`templates/cattle/`](cattle/index.html) | Leather and wood: pale maple by day, dark oiled leather by night. Headings branded into the board in Alfa Slab One with a scorched halo, leather chrome with a running stitch, brass rivets, a steer mark on the page break, Bitter for the body. Example: Harlan Creek, a smokehouse. |
| Termy | [`templates/termy/`](termy/index.html) | A terminal: dark green screen with phosphor text, glow and scanlines by day, the same phosphor on true black by night. JetBrains Mono, no corners, brackets round buttons, prompts before kickers, a blinking cursor, and a CRT overlay with a strength knob. Example: shelf, a notes CLI. |
