Component

Flare

Flare is everything a template adds beyond the contract. Cattle adds four things from the smokehouse: the brand as a utility class, the leather panel, the maker's mark, and a menu list. Three more are variants of contract components and live on their own pages: the stitched card, the rivet button and the rope rule; the leather fifty-fifty is the panel applied to that block. The burn itself is two tokens, --cattle-char and --cattle-burn, and every h1 and h2 already carries it.

The brand

Put cattle-brand on anything and it is burnt in the way h1 and h2 are: the slab, capitals, the char fill and the scorch halo. By day the halo is four rings of brown fading into the grain; by night the same list of shadows is a dark press and a pale catch, so the lettering reads as tooled into the leather. One list of shadows, both halves, no data-scheme hook. Use it on a card title, a price, a sign; not on running text.

<p class="cattle-brand">Open at eleven. Gone when it's gone.</p>

The leather panel

Put cattle-leather on a block and it is saddle leather with a running stitch inside the edge. Inside it the semantic tokens are re-pointed: text is cream, links and kickers are thread, a primary button is thread on leather, and burnt headings become tooled ones. The navbar, the sidebar, the footer and the author card are the same panel; this is the class that lets you add another. It takes its own padding on a plain block and none on a .fifty-fifty, a .card or an .accordion-group, which have their own.

<div class="cattle-flare">
  <div class="cattle-leather">
    <p class="kicker">Hours</p>
    <h2>Eleven until it runs out</h2>
    <p>Brisket comes off at eleven and again at four. When the second one is gone, we close. <a href="#">The menu</a> says what there is today.</p>
    <a class="btn btn-primary" href="#">Book a table</a>
  </div>
</div>

The maker's mark

A steer head, drawn from the --cattle-mark token as a mask and filled with the char colour, scorched by day. It is an empty span, so mark it aria-hidden. The overview puts one above the hero headline; the example puts one on the home page. Change the token in theme.css and your own mark appears here, on every page break and on the feature card.

<div class="cattle-flare"><span class="cattle-mark" aria-hidden="true"></span></div>

The menu list

A list of names and prices with a dotted leader between them and a line of description under each. The price is burnt. It is a ul with three spans per item, so without the stylesheet it is still a readable list; the wrapper is cattle-flare, so a template swap can remove the whole thing. The example menu is four of these.

<div class="cattle-flare">
  <ul class="cattle-menu">
    <li><span class="cattle-menu-name">Brisket</span><span class="cattle-menu-price">18</span><span class="cattle-menu-note">Fourteen hours over post oak. Sliced when you order.</span></li>
    <li><span class="cattle-menu-name">Pork ribs</span><span class="cattle-menu-price">16</span><span class="cattle-menu-note">Half a rack, dry rubbed.</span></li>
    <li><span class="cattle-menu-name">Pit beans</span><span class="cattle-menu-price">5</span><span class="cattle-menu-note">Cooked under the brisket.</span></li>
  </ul>
</div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == flare == */, except the leather panel's token re-pointing, which is under /* == navbar == */ because the chrome shares it, and the three variants, which sit with their components.

ClassPurpose
.cattle-flareThe wrapper for flare that is markup; strip it when swapping templates
.cattle-brandBurns any element: slab, capitals, char fill, scorch halo
.cattle-leatherA stitched leather panel; re-points the semantic tokens inside it
.cattle-markThe steer mark from the --cattle-mark token, 4rem wide, scorched
.cattle-menuThe list; each li is a three-column grid with the leader drawn by ::after
.cattle-menu-name, -price, -noteThe three spans: bold name, burnt price, muted note on the second row
.card.cattle-stitchedLeather frame with the stitch through it (global.css § cards)
.btn.cattle-rivetLeather strap, stitch, two brass rivets (global.css § buttons)
hr.cattle-ropeTwo strands of hemp rope (global.css § page-break)

Notes

  • Every colour here is a token in theme.css: --cattle-char, the four --cattle-scorch-* rings, --cattle-burn, --cattle-tooled, the --cattle-leather-* set, --cattle-thread, the --cattle-brass-* set, --cattle-rivet, --cattle-rope-*, --cattle-grain, --cattle-leather, --cattle-veil and --cattle-mark. Nothing in global.css names a colour.
  • The only motion in the template is the burn-in on the feature card, the rivet marker on an accordion and the press of a button, all transitions the global prefers-reduced-motion rule shortens to nothing. The grain and the leather are still textures, not animations.
  • The brand and the menu are content and should not be hidden from assistive technology. The mark is decoration; mark it aria-hidden="true" as the example does.
  • Alfa Slab One has one weight and no italic. The brand uses it at that weight; do not put cattle-brand on text that asks for bold or italic.
  • The leather panel's stitch is an outline, so it is not clipped by overflow: hidden and it survives on a .card. Do not give a stitched element a focus style of its own; focus goes to the controls inside it.
