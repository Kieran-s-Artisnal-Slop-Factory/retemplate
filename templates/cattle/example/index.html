Harlan Creek Smokehouse · since 1998

We light the fire at four.

Post oak, salt, pepper and time. The brisket comes off when it is done, not when the clock says, and when the second one is gone we close. Eleven till it runs out, Wednesday to Sunday.

What comes off the pit

Brisket

Whole packer cuts, fourteen hours. We slice it in front of you; ask for point or flat and you will get it.

Ribs and shoulder

Pork ribs dry rubbed, shoulder pulled by hand. Sauce is on the table. Nobody will mind if you use it.

By the pound

Everything on the board goes by weight to take away, wrapped in butcher paper. The prices.

The pit

One fire, two pits, no gas

Both pits are offset steel, built in the yard in 1998 and patched every winter since. The wood is post oak from the Harlan place up the creek, seasoned a year. There is no thermostat; there is a man with a chair.

Asked at the counter

What time should I come?

Before noon if you want brisket for certain. The second brisket comes off at four and usually lasts until six. Saturdays it does not.

Do you take bookings?

For tables of six or more, and for anyone on a Friday or Saturday evening. Under six on a weekday, walk in. The booking form takes a minute.

Is there anything for people who do not eat meat?

Smoked cauliflower with the same rub, pit beans made without the drippings if you ask, and every side. It is a smokehouse; it is not a trap.

!

Closed Sunday 14 September. The pits are being relined and the yard will be full of firebrick. Back on the Wednesday with the usual board.
