The board

The menu

Plates come with two sides and white bread. Everything is smoked over post oak and nothing is fried. When something runs out it comes off the board; the board is right.

From the pit

  • Brisket plate18Fourteen hours. Sliced to order; say point, flat or both.
  • Rib plate16Half a rack of pork ribs, dry rubbed. Sauce on the table.
  • Pulled shoulder plate15Pulled by hand, dressed with the drippings.
  • Half chicken14Skin on. Comes off at eleven and again at four.
  • Sausage plate13Two links, coarse ground, made Tuesday.
  • Three-meat plate22Any three of the above. Bring someone.
  • Smoked cauliflower plate13A whole head, the same rub, four hours.

Sides

Two come with a plate. Extra sides are the price shown.

  • Pit beans5Cooked under the brisket so they catch what falls. Ask for them without.
  • Slaw4Vinegar, not mayonnaise.
  • Potato salad4Mustard, dill, a lot of black pepper.
  • Cornbread3In the skillet, with the burnt edge.
  • Pickles and onion2Our own pickles. The onion is just an onion.

Sweet and to drink

  • Banana pudding6Made in the morning. Gone by three.
  • Pecan pie6By the slice. Whole pies with two days' notice.
  • Sweet tea3Refills are free. Unsweet on request.
  • Beer5Two on tap, both from the county. Ask which.
  • Creek water0From the tap, not the creek. Help yourself.

By the pound

Everything goes by weight to take away, wrapped in butcher paper, with bread and pickles thrown in. Whole briskets and whole shoulders need a day's notice: ring, or ask at the counter.
