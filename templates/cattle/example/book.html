House rules

Order at the counter

Even with a table. Line up, say what you want, watch it sliced, carry the tray. Drinks are at the end; refills are on you.

Fifteen minutes

A held table goes to the line fifteen minutes after the time you gave us. Ring if you are stuck behind a tractor; we will do what we can.

When it is gone, it is gone

A booking holds a table, not a brisket. If you want brisket for certain, book eleven or four. We will not put a cold one in the microwave for you and you would not want us to.

Dogs, children, boots

All welcome in the yard. Dogs on a lead near the pits. Children may look at the fire from the fence and no closer.
