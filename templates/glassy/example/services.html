Questions clients ask

Do you work with agencies?

Yes, as long as we can talk to the client directly. We do not do white-label work.

What if the scope changes?

We re-plan and re-price the difference, in writing, before doing it. Small changes are usually free; we will tell you which is which.

Can you host it?

We would rather you did, on a service you control. We will set it up and show you where everything is.

Do you do shops?

Small ones, yes: Hartsop Wool and Grizedale Glass are both ours. Anything with a warehouse is a different kind of job and we will say so.

The first conversation

An hour, free, on a call or in the studio. We will send a calendar invite.
