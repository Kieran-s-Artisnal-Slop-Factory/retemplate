How a project goes

A conversation

An hour, free, on a call or in the studio. We ask what the site is for and who it is for. If we are not the right fit we will say so and suggest someone who is.

A plan and a price

One page: what we will make, when, and what it costs. Fixed. Half on signing, half on launch.

Design, build, launch

Weekly shares of real, working pages rather than pictures of pages. You see the site grow and steer it as it does.

Afterwards

A handover with your team, the source in your repository, and a month of fixes included. Then a retainer if you want one, or nothing at all.
