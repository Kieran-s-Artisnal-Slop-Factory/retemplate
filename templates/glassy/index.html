Glass, in three depths

Colour behind. Glass in front.

Two blurred orbs drift behind the page. Every card, bar and dialog is a sheet of frosted glass over them, and the sheets sit at three depths, so a page is built by layering rather than by stacking. Same markup as every other template here.

Get started See the studio site

Shallow

A third of the light, a soft blur, no shadow. For things that should sit back: a note, a list of people, the address.

Sheet

The default. Sixty percent white by day, half navy by night, a 24px blur and a one-pixel bright edge. Every component starts here.

Deep

Nearly opaque, a 40px blur, a lifted shadow and a white edge. For the one thing on the page you should read first.

What is in the folder

Two sets of files. The install set is the template; the showcase is these pages, and you delete it.

Keep

css/theme.css holds the tokens and the glass recipe; css/global.css holds every component; js/theme.js is the scheme toggle; assets/ is the art.

Delete

The four top-level pages, docs/, example/ and js/copy.js. Or keep example/ and start from the studio site.

The studio site

The example is Osier, a design studio of four in Kendal. Its home page is a deep sheet over the orbs, its services are layered panels with the prices in a glass table, and its contact page is one frosted card.

Eight sheets

Glass over eight different backdrops. Click one; the lightbox is a <dialog> on deep glass, the arrows are links, and Esc closes it.

Questions

Accordions are native <details>. Give a group one name and opening one closes the others, with no script.

What makes it read as glass?

Three things: a translucent fill, a backdrop-filter that blurs whatever is behind it, and a one-pixel edge brighter than the fill. Every sheet here has all three, and the depth tiers only change how much of each.

Does the blur cost anything?

A little on old hardware. Every sheet pays for one blur. On a page with hundreds of cards, lower --glassy-blur in theme.css, or mark the cards glassy-shallow, which uses a 10px blur.

Which browsers?

Anything from 2024 on. The template relies on light-dark(), :has(), popovers, exclusive accordions and backdrop-filter, with no polyfills.

Does the palette work in retoken?

Yes. The palette block in theme.css pastes into a retoken site as it is. The glass, the orbs and the waves are this template's own tokens and stay here. See the tokens.

Small parts, on one sheet

A pane is a section-sized sheet for grouping smaller glass. Buttons are pills; the ghost button is the accent as an outline. The switch thumb is the accent. Badges are small pills of deep glass.

A modal dialog

Words

Most pages are prose, so .prose styles every construct a markdown renderer produces; code blocks are small sheets of their own. The blog post runs the full test document through it, and defaults shows every bare element.

Glass is only glass if you can see something through it. Put colour behind it, and keep the sheets few.
