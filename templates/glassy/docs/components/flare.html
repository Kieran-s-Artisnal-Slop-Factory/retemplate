Flare

Flare is everything a template adds beyond the contract. Glassy adds the two things that make glassmorphism read as glass: colour behind the panes, and something moving behind them.

Orbs

Two large blurred discs, fixed behind the page, drifting slowly. Put the block right after <body>; it is aria-hidden and never intercepts a click. (The preview below is inert because it sits inside the page rather than behind it.)

<div class="glassy-flare glassy-orbs" aria-hidden="true">
  <div class="glassy-orb"></div>
  <div class="glassy-orb"></div>
</div>

Waves

One path, four <use> copies, four different durations. Because the path has an id, use the block once per page.

<div class="glassy-flare glassy-waves" aria-hidden="true">
  <svg viewBox="0 24 150 28" preserveAspectRatio="none">
    <defs><path id="glassy-wave" d="M-160 44c30 0 58-18 88-18s58 18 88 18 58-18 88-18 58 18 88 18v44h-352z"/></defs>
    <g class="glassy-wave-set">
      <use href="#glassy-wave" x="48" y="0"/>
      <use href="#glassy-wave" x="48" y="3"/>
      <use href="#glassy-wave" x="48" y="5"/>
      <use href="#glassy-wave" x="48" y="7"/>
    </g>
  </svg>
</div>

A glass panel

The recipe every component uses, as a free-standing block.

<div class="glassy-panel">
  <p class="kicker">A glass panel</p>
  <p>Any block of content can sit on a sheet of glass. This is the same recipe every card, banner and table in the template uses.</p>
</div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == flare == */.

ClassPurpose
.glassy-orbsFixed, full-viewport, behind everything
.glassy-orbA blurred disc; the two children take the two orb tokens
.glassy-wavesA clipped strip holding the SVG
.glassy-wave-set useOne wave layer; delays and durations differ per child
.glassy-panelGlass background, bright edge, blur, large radius
.glassy-flareThe wrapper for flare that is markup

Notes

  • The gradient, the glass, the edge, the blur radius, the wave colour and the orb colours are all tokens in theme.css (--glassy-*). Nothing in global.css names a colour.
  • Both animations are switched off by prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.
  • backdrop-filter is what makes the glass frosted. Every panel pays for one; on very long pages with hundreds of cards, lower --glassy-blur.
