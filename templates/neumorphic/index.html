Soft UI

Everything is one surface. Light does the rest.

Neumorphic draws every card, button and field out of the page colour with a pair of shadows. Nothing has a border; things you can press stand proud, and things you type into sink in.

What you get

Take the css/, js/theme.js and assets/ folders and you have the whole template. The three cards below are the ordinary card, with an image, an icon, and neither; each one is the same colour as the page and only the shadows tell you it is there.

Two shadow colours

A light and a dark, set once in theme.css. Every raised and sunken shape on the site is built from them.

Light and dark

By day the surface is a cool grey with white highlights; by night it is charcoal with deep shadows. The toggle in the corner cycles auto, light and dark.

Retoken compatible

The palette block in theme.css pastes straight into a retoken site. See the tokens.

Questions, folded

Accordions are native <details> elements. Give a group of them the same name and opening one closes the others, with no script.

Does this need a build step?

No. Copy the folder, link the two stylesheets and the one script, and write HTML.

Where are the borders?

There are none. Every edge is a shadow, which is what makes the surfaces feel moulded rather than drawn. The one exception is the coloured edge on a banner.

Which browsers?

Anything from 2024 on: the template relies on light-dark(), :has(), popovers and exclusive accordions.

A single accordion stands on its own.

What is in the install set?

css/, js/theme.js and assets/. The README lists it.

A gallery with a lightbox

Click any image. The lightbox is a <dialog>; the arrows and the thumbnails are plain links, and Esc closes it.

Words

Prose is what most pages are made of, so .prose styles every construct a markdown renderer produces. Code blocks are sunken wells; quotes keep a quiet rule. The blog post page runs the full test document through it; defaults shows every bare element.

Depth is a property of light, not of lines. Take the lines away and the light is still there.
