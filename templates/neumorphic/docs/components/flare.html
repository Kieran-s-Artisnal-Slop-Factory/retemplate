Flare

Flare is everything a template adds beyond the contract. Neumorphic's additions are all controls, because soft UI is about things you can press: a raised panel that groups controls, a sunken well, a dial that reads a value and a segmented choice. Four more are variants of contract components and live on their own pages: the tile and the pressed card, the round button and the sunken banner.

The panel

A raised board, the first thing on the overview and on every example page. Its grid puts a dial in the first column and a stack of controls in the second; a child with neumorphic-panel-row spans both. Below 30rem it becomes one centred column. It sits inside the .neumorphic-flare wrapper because a template swap has nothing to put in its place.

<div class="neumorphic-flare neumorphic-panel">
  <div class="neumorphic-dial" style="--angle: 200deg" role="img" aria-label="Target temperature 21 degrees"><span class="neumorphic-dial-value">21°</span><span class="neumorphic-dial-label">target</span></div>
  <div class="stack">
    <label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" checked><span>Heating</span></label>
    <label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch"><span>Hot water boost</span></label>
  </div>
  <div class="neumorphic-panel-row">
    <div class="neumorphic-segment" role="radiogroup" aria-label="Mode">
      <input type="radio" id="fl-away" name="fl-mode"><label for="fl-away">Away</label>
      <input type="radio" id="fl-home" name="fl-mode" checked><label for="fl-home">Home</label>
      <input type="radio" id="fl-night" name="fl-mode"><label for="fl-night">Night</label>
    </div>
    <div class="row">
      <button class="btn neumorphic-round" type="button" aria-label="Cooler">−</button>
      <button class="btn neumorphic-round" type="button" aria-label="Warmer">+</button>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

The dial

A raised disc with a sunken face and a pointer. Set the pointer with the --angle custom property, where 0deg is straight up; the value and its label are two spans in the middle. It does not turn: it reads. Give it role="img" and an aria-label that says what it shows. neumorphic-dial-lg makes it bigger for a page about one thing.

<div class="row">
  <div class="neumorphic-dial" style="--angle: 200deg" role="img" aria-label="Target temperature 21 degrees"><span class="neumorphic-dial-value">21°</span><span class="neumorphic-dial-label">target</span></div>
  <div class="neumorphic-dial neumorphic-dial-lg" style="--angle: 120deg" role="img" aria-label="Battery 62 percent"><span class="neumorphic-dial-value">62%</span><span class="neumorphic-dial-label">battery</span></div>
</div>

The segment

A group of radio buttons in a sunken track; the checked one is a raised tab. It is the control for one choice among a few, and the labels say what the reader is choosing. Inside a form it submits like any radio group. Give the wrapper role="radiogroup" and an aria-label, or put it in a .field under a .field-label as the forms page shows.

<div class="neumorphic-segment" role="radiogroup" aria-label="Heating">
  <input type="radio" id="seg-off" name="seg-heat"><label for="seg-off">Off</label>
  <input type="radio" id="seg-eco" name="seg-heat"><label for="seg-eco">Eco</label>
  <input type="radio" id="seg-comfort" name="seg-heat" checked><label for="seg-comfort">Comfort</label>
</div>

The well

A pressed-in panel for content that belongs together. Put it on a <form> or a <fieldset> and the fields inside sit in a sunken group, which is how the example's settings page is built.

<div class="neumorphic-well">
  <div class="row">
    <label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" checked><span>Lights</span></label>
    <label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch"><span>Heating</span></label>
    <button class="btn btn-sm" type="button">Apply</button>
  </div>
</div>

Notes

  • The shadow recipes are tokens in theme.css (--neumorphic-raise, --neumorphic-inset and friends), built from two colours, --neumorphic-light and --neumorphic-dark. Change those two and the whole template follows.
  • The dial is static. If a value changes, change the text and the --angle in the same place; the label for assistive technology is the aria-label, so change that too.
  • The segment's radios are visually hidden but stay in the tab order; the focus ring lands on the label.
  • Nothing here animates beyond a short transition, so there is nothing to pause for reduced motion.
