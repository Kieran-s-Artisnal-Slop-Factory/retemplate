Flare

Flare is everything a template adds beyond the contract. Neumorphic adds two things, both made of nothing but shadow: a sunken well for grouping controls, and a raised dial for decoration.

The well

Content that belongs together sits in a pressed-in panel. It is the one place the template uses an inset shadow at page scale.

<div class="neumorphic-well">
  <p class="kicker">A sunken group</p>
  <div class="row">
    <label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" checked><span>Lights</span></label>
    <label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch"><span>Heating</span></label>
    <button class="btn btn-sm" type="button">Apply</button>
  </div>
</div>

The dial

Purely decorative, so it is aria-hidden and lives inside the .neumorphic-flare wrapper that a template swap strips.

<div class="neumorphic-flare neumorphic-dials">
  <div class="neumorphic-knob" aria-hidden="true"></div>
  <div class="neumorphic-knob" aria-hidden="true"></div>
</div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == flare == */.

ClassPurpose
.neumorphic-wellInset panel: padding, large radius, inset shadow
.neumorphic-knobA raised disc with a sunken face and an accent pointer
.neumorphic-dialsCentres a row of knobs
.neumorphic-flareThe wrapper for flare that is markup, so a swap can strip it

Notes

  • The shadow recipes are tokens in theme.css (--neumorphic-raise, --neumorphic-inset and friends), built from two colours, --neumorphic-light and --neumorphic-dark. Change those two and the whole template follows.
  • Nothing here animates beyond a short transition, so there is nothing to pause for reduced motion.
