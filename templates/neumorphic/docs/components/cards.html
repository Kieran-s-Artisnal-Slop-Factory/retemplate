Cards

A bordered surface with optional media, a title and text. Vertical by default; horizontal with a class; and one feature card per template with an effect that suits the design.

Preview

  <figure class="card-media"><img src="../../assets/card-1.svg" alt="" width="640" height="400"></figure>
  <div class="card-body">
    <h3 class="card-title">Card title</h3>
    <p class="card-text">A sentence or two of description under the title.</p>
  </div>
</article>

Tile, Neumorphic's own

A card that reads one number. Add neumorphic-tile to the card and put the reading in a .neumorphic-tile-value; a <small> inside it is the unit. The title becomes a small label above the number.

<div class="grid-2">
  <article class="card neumorphic-tile">
    <div class="card-body">
      <h3 class="card-title">Battery</h3>
      <p class="neumorphic-tile-value">62 <small>%</small></p>
      <p class="card-text">Enough for the evening.</p>
    </div>
  </article>
  <article class="card neumorphic-tile">
    <div class="card-body">
      <h3 class="card-title">Outside</h3>
      <p class="neumorphic-tile-value">9 <small>°</small></p>
      <p class="card-text">Falling to 4° overnight.</p>
    </div>
  </article>
</div>

Pressed, Neumorphic's own

The same card sunk into the surface. Use it for what is off, idle or not chosen, next to raised cards for what is on; the picture dims a little and the title goes muted.

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == cards == */.

ClassPurpose
.cardThe surface: border, radius, shadow, flex column
.card-mediaFigure holding an image or an inline SVG
.card-iconAn inline SVG sized and coloured as an icon
.card-bodyPadding and vertical spacing
.card-titleThe heading
.card-textMuted description
.card-actionsButtons at the bottom, pushed down
.card-horizontalMedia beside the body
.card-media-endWith .card-horizontal: media on the right
.card-featureThe template's dynamic card
.neumorphic-tileNeumorphic: a card that reads one number; the title becomes a label
.neumorphic-tile-valueNeumorphic: the reading, large and tabular; <small> for the unit
.neumorphic-pressedNeumorphic: the card sunk into the surface

Notes

  • Cards are <article> elements; use <div> if the content is not self-contained.
  • Lay them out with .grid-2 or .grid-3; they stretch to equal heights.
  • Horizontal cards stack on screens narrower than 30rem.
  • The feature card responds to :hover and :focus-within; it is CSS only.
