Hero

The opening of a page: a heading at the largest size, a paragraph, one or two buttons, and beside them whatever the template leads with. Every template has its own hero form; this page shows Neumorphic's, then a second variant that moves.

Preview

The hero as the overview uses it. The heading takes --font-size-hero; the paragraph is muted and capped at a readable measure.

<section class="hero">
  <div class="stack">
    <h1>One surface, two shadows.</h1>
    <p>Nothing on this page is drawn with a line. What you can press stands out of the grey; what you can set sinks into it. The panel beside this is live: every control on it is the markup you get.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
      <a class="btn btn-lg" href="../../example/index.html">See the house</a>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="neumorphic-flare neumorphic-panel">
    <div class="neumorphic-dial" style="--angle: 200deg" role="img" aria-label="Target temperature 21 degrees"><span class="neumorphic-dial-value">21°</span><span class="neumorphic-dial-label">target</span></div>
    <div class="stack">
      <label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" checked><span>Heating</span></label>
      <label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" checked><span>Porch light</span></label>
      <label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch"><span>Hot water boost</span></label>
    </div>

</section>

Variants

The breath

The hero becomes a raised plate that breathes: over six seconds its shadow softens until it lies flat in the surface, then it rises again. The panel inside breathes the other way, sinking as the plate rises, so the two trade depth. Add neumorphic-breathe to the hero. Under prefers-reduced-motion the global rule cuts every animation to nothing, so the hero is simply there.

<section class="hero neumorphic-breathe">
  <div class="stack">
    <h1>The surface breathes.</h1>
    <p>Watch the edge of this plate. It rises out of the grey, settles flat, and rises again, and the panel beside it does the opposite.</p>
    <div class="row">
      <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
      <a class="btn btn-lg" href="../../example/index.html">See the house</a>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="neumorphic-flare neumorphic-panel">
    <div class="neumorphic-dial" style="--angle: 200deg" role="img" aria-label="Target temperature 21 degrees"><span class="neumorphic-dial-value">21°</span><span class="neumorphic-dial-label">target</span></div>
    <div class="stack">
      <label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" checked><span>Heating</span></label>
      <label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch"><span>Hot water boost</span></label>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css: the hero under /* == utilities == */ with the other layout helpers, the variant under /* == hero variants == */.

ClassPurpose
.heroThe opening section: a grid of a text stack and, in most templates, a picture or a piece of flare
.hero .stackThe kicker, heading, paragraph and button row
.hero .rowThe buttons
.hero.neumorphic-breatheThe plate: raised, then flat, then raised, on a six-second loop
.neumorphic-breathe .neumorphic-panelBreathes the opposite way, flat to inset

Notes

  • One <h1> per page: the hero's heading is it. The kicker above it is a <p>, not a heading.
  • Above 48rem the hero is two columns, text then picture; below, it stacks. The variant keeps whatever layout its markup implies.
  • The variant is CSS only. Its animation is declared with both fill, so the page is correct before it starts and after it ends, and the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.
  • Another template ignores neumorphic-breathe and shows its own hero; any flare markup inside sits in the template's flare wrapper so a swap can drop it.
