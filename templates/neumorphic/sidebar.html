The overview, with a sidebar

Every template ships two layouts. The navbar layout is on the overview, the blog post and the forms page; this page and the docs use the sidebar layout. In Neumorphic the sidebar is not a panel with a border but the page itself, pressed a little forward by a soft shadow. The controls below are the same ones the overview opens with.

21°target

What the layout is made of

A grid with two rows and, above 64rem, two columns. The top bar spans both columns and stays put; the sidebar is a sticky column with its own scrollbar; the page scrolls under it. Below 64rem the sidebar carries the popover attribute, so the menu button is a real button that opens it, and the browser supplies the dimmed backdrop and the dismiss behaviour.

How it is built
Can the sidebar collapse on a wide screen?

Not in this version. The drawer behaviour is for narrow screens only.

Why are there no borders anywhere?

Because the shadows do the work. Every edge you see is light falling on a shape, not a line drawn around it.
