Front page · The template

Set loud. Ruled thick. Never rounded.

Brutal News is a newspaper that never learned to whisper. Every box is ruled in three pixels of ink, the headlines are Archivo Black at the largest size the page will hold, and the palette is what you find in a newsagent's window: hot red, acid yellow, cobalt, signal green. Shadows are hard offsets or nothing.

Underneath the shouting it is the same template as every other one in retemplate: the same markup, the same components, the same two stylesheets and one small script. Swap css/ and assets/ for another template's and the pages change their clothes.

What this one adds is the furniture of a paper. A masthead with a dateline. Story cards with kickers and bylines. A scoreboard table. A breaking banner. A pull quote set between rules. Datelines and data in JetBrains Mono, body in Inter, in real columns.

Picture · The rule

Three pixels, and no less

One pixel is a hairline and two is a border. Three is a rule: the line a paper draws between two things it wants you to read separately. Every border, underline, column rule and table rule in this template is three pixels or six.

Card docs

In brief

Accordions are native <details> elements. Give a group the same name and opening one closes the others, with no script. The marker is a plus that turns.

Build step: none

Copy the folder, link the two stylesheets and the one script, and write HTML. The pages open from disk.

Fonts: three, from Google

Archivo Black for headlines, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for kickers, datelines and data. Remove the <link> and Arial Black, your system sans and your system mono take over; the rules carry the look on their own.

Browsers: 2024 on

The template relies on light-dark(), :has(), popovers and exclusive accordions. No polyfills.

A single accordion stands on its own.

What is in the install set?

css/, js/theme.js, assets/ and the README. Everything else on this page is showcase and can go.

Picture library

Eight pictures from the stock cupboard. Click one and the lightbox opens; the arrows are plain links, the close button is a form, and Esc closes it.

Small print

Buttons are mono capitals in a ruled box and jump towards you when hovered; the stamp is Brutal News's own, a red outline that fills. The switch is a square slider. Badges are stamps of a smaller kind; banners are ruled notices with a thick left edge.

Notices

Dialogs are native. The button opens one; the dialog closes itself through a form, traps focus while open and dims the page behind it.

Place a notice

Births, marriages, deaths, lost cats. Forty words for two pounds; the desk sets it in the Thursday paper. This dialog closes on Esc, and all of that comes from the browser.

Letters

Most pages are prose, so .prose styles every construct a markdown renderer produces: headings ruled off, lists, quotes, code, tables, footnotes. Quotes in running text are set in the display face behind a red rule; the pull quote below is the flare version, between a heavy rule and a plain one. The blog post runs the full test document through these rules; defaults shows every bare element.

A rule is a line that means something. A hairline is a line that is afraid to.
