Cards

A bordered surface with optional media, a title and text. Vertical by default; horizontal with a class; and one feature card per template with an effect that suits the design.

Preview

  <figure class="card-media"><img src="../../assets/card-1.svg" alt="" width="640" height="400"></figure>
  <div class="card-body">
    <h3 class="card-title">Card title</h3>
    <p class="card-text">A sentence or two of description under the title.</p>
  </div>
</article>

Story card

Add brutal-news-story and the card stops being a box. A heavy rule on top, the picture ruled on all four sides, a red kicker, the headline a size larger in Archivo Black, and a byline at the foot over a rule. Inside .card-body the kicker (.kicker) and the byline (.brutal-news-byline) are ordinary paragraphs; another template ignores the class and shows a plain card with two extra lines. Lay story cards in a .grid-3.brutal-news-ruled to rule them off from each other, as the overview does.

The flash

A breaking-story card: the kicker inverts to red and back in hard steps, twice a second, and nothing eases. Use it for the one story that is happening now; two on a page and neither is news. Add brutal-news-flash to the card; the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.

<article class="card brutal-news-story brutal-news-flash">
  <figure class="card-media"><img src="../../assets/card-1.svg" alt="" width="640" height="400"></figure>
  <div class="card-body">
    <p class="kicker">Breaking</p>
    <h3 class="card-title">The kicker will not sit still</h3>
    <p class="card-text">Red on white, then white on red, with no frame in between. It stops under reduced motion and stays red.</p>
  </div>
</article>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == cards == */.

ClassPurpose
.cardThe surface: border, radius, shadow, flex column
.card-mediaFigure holding an image or an inline SVG
.card-iconAn inline SVG sized and coloured as an icon
.card-bodyPadding and vertical spacing
.card-titleThe heading
.card-textMuted description
.card-actionsButtons at the bottom, pushed down
.card-horizontalMedia beside the body
.card-media-endWith .card-horizontal: media on the right
.card-featureThe template's dynamic card: inverts to ink on red and throws a hard shadow
.brutal-news-storyBrutal News only: no box, heavy rule on top, red kicker, byline at the foot (rules under /* == flare == */)
.brutal-news-bylineMono capitals over a rule; the last line of a story card
.card.brutal-news-flashThe kicker cuts between red on paper and paper on red, twice a second

Notes

  • Cards are <article> elements; use <div> if the content is not self-contained.
  • Lay them out with .grid-2 or .grid-3; they stretch to equal heights.
  • Horizontal cards stack on screens narrower than 30rem.
  • The feature card responds to :hover and :focus-within; it is CSS only. The inversion is a background and a colour on the card and its media; the shadow is --shadow-2, a hard offset.
  • .brutal-news-story is not portable: another template shows an ordinary card, which is the intended fallback.
