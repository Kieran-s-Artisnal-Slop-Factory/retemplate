Flare

Flare is everything a template adds beyond the contract. Brutal News adds the furniture of a newspaper: the lead, a masthead and its dateline, ruled columns with a drop cap, a ruled grid, a pull quote, a byline, a ruled box and a red block. The variants of contract components (the story card, the scoreboard table, the stamp button, the breaking banner) are documented on those components' pages and listed at the foot of this one.

The lead

The front-page story, and the hero of this template. Add brutal-news-lead to .hero: a red kicker, the headline across the full width at the largest size on the page (Archivo Black, tracked to minus four and a half percent), then the text in three ruled columns with a drop cap, a byline, and one call. Two columns below 64rem, one below 40rem. There is no picture; the headline is the picture. The overview and the example front page both open with one, and the example story reuses it as a .post-header.

<section class="hero brutal-news-lead">
  <p class="kicker">Council · Exclusive</p>
  <h1>Baths shut in March. Council knew in May.</h1>
  <div class="brutal-news-columns">
    <p class="brutal-news-dropcap">Ashcombe Baths will close on 2 March for at least eighteen months, the council confirmed last night, after the Bugle obtained a structural survey describing the roof trusses as "beyond economic repair". The survey is dated 11 May.</p>
    <p>Councillors were briefed in June. The swimming club, which trains there five mornings a week, found out on Monday when a notice went up by the lockers.</p>
    <p>Repair is costed at £4.1m; a new pool at £11m. The council has budgeted for neither.</p>
  </div>
  <p class="brutal-news-byline">By Marta Okonkwo, chief reporter</p>
  <div class="row"><a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="#">Read the full story</a></div>
</section>

Masthead

The paper's name between a heavy rule and the dateline. It is markup, so it lives inside the .brutal-news-flare wrapper a template swap strips. Keep the name to three words; it is set at up to 4.5rem.

<div class="brutal-news-flare">
  <header class="brutal-news-masthead">
    <p class="kicker">Ashcombe's weekly since 1898</p>
    <h1>The Ashcombe Bugle</h1>
    <p class="brutal-news-dateline"><span>No. 6,614</span><span>Thursday 4 September 2026</span><span>Bright, 19°</span><span>£1.50</span></p>
  </header>
</div>

Dateline

Mono facts spread across the width, ruled top and bottom. Inside the masthead it is the bottom rule; on its own it opens an inside page. Every span is a fact: an edition number, a date, a price, the weather. It is not for decoration.

<div class="brutal-news-flare">
  <p class="brutal-news-dateline"><span>News · Page 4</span><span>Thursday 4 September 2026</span><span>No. 6,614</span></p>
</div>

Columns and a drop cap

Two columns above 40rem with a rule between them; one below. The drop cap is ::first-letter on the first paragraph. Inside .brutal-news-lead the count rises to three.

<div class="brutal-news-columns">
  <p class="brutal-news-dropcap">Newspapers learned a long time ago that a page is easier to read in columns. The measure stays short, the eye never travels far, and a rule between the columns keeps the two from bleeding into each other.</p>
  <p>The drop cap is generated by the stylesheet from the first letter of the first paragraph; there is nothing extra in the markup.</p>
</div>

Ruled grid

Add brutal-news-ruled to .grid-2 or .grid-3 and the gaps become rules: a heavy one across the top, a plain one between the columns. Below 40rem the rules turn horizontal. The overview uses it under story cards.

<div class="grid-3 brutal-news-ruled">
  <div><p class="kicker">Allotments</p><p>Waiting list hits nine years at Mill Field.</p></div>
  <div><p class="kicker">Sport</p><p>Town lose 3-0 at home, blame the pitch.</p></div>
  <div><p class="kicker">Planning</p><p>Forty flats proposed for the old maltings.</p></div>
</div>

Pull quote

A <blockquote> set in the display face between a heavy rule and a plain one, with a red opening quote mark and the source in a <footer>. Running-text quotes in .prose keep the red left rule; this is for the one line you want read before the story.

<blockquote class="brutal-news-pullquote">
  <p>Two winters is not a forecast. It is a deadline the council chose not to mention.</p>
  <footer>Tom Achebe, Ashcombe Swimming Club</footer>
</blockquote>

Byline

Mono capitals over a rule. It follows the headline in a story and closes a story card. Use it for names and dates, not slogans.

<p class="brutal-news-byline">By Marta Okonkwo, chief reporter · Additional reporting by Rhys Calloway</p>

Ruled box

A heavy border, a hard shadow, and its label in acid yellow with black ink. This is the only place the yellow appears under text, and the text on it is always black. Use it for the numbers, a notice, an advert.

<div class="brutal-news-box">
  <p class="kicker">In numbers</p>
  <p><strong>116</strong> days between the survey and the notice · <strong>210</strong> club members · <strong>14</strong> miles to the next pool</p>
</div>

Red block

Solid brand colour with paper-coloured type, for the one section on a page that has to shout: the offer, the appeal, the reverse-print note. A button inside it prints in reverse too.

<div class="brutal-news-red">
  <h2>Two pounds a month. No paywall.</h2>
  <p>The Bugle is paid for by the people who read it.</p>
  <a class="btn" href="#">Subscribe</a>
</div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == flare == */. The last four rows are variants of contract components; their rules are in the same section and their examples on the pages named.

ClassPurpose
.brutal-news-flareThe wrapper for flare that is markup; strip it when swapping templates
.hero.brutal-news-leadThe front-page story: full-width headline, three ruled columns, byline
.brutal-news-mastheadThe paper's name, heavy rule above, dateline below, centred
.brutal-news-datelineMono facts spread across the width, ruled top and bottom
.brutal-news-columnsTwo ruled columns; one below 40rem; three inside the lead
.brutal-news-dropcapFloats the first letter, four lines tall
.brutal-news-ruledOn .grid-2 or .grid-3: rules instead of gaps
.brutal-news-pullquoteDisplay type between rules, red opening quote, source in a footer
.brutal-news-bylineMono capitals over a rule
.brutal-news-boxHeavy border, hard shadow, yellow label
.brutal-news-redSolid brand colour, paper-coloured type
.card.brutal-news-storyNo box, heavy rule on top, red kicker, byline (cards)
.table-wrap.brutal-news-scoreboardMono figures right-aligned, head in reverse (table)
.btn.brutal-news-stampRed outline in display type, fills on hover (buttons)
.banner.brutal-news-breakingSolid red, first child as a label (banner)

Notes

  • The rule weights are two tokens, --brutal-news-rule (3px) and --brutal-news-rule-heavy (6px). Nothing in the template draws thinner than the first. The yellow and the red are tokens too, so nothing in global.css names a colour.
  • Nothing animates. The only motion in the template is the 90ms jump on buttons, gallery tiles and the feature card, and that is shortened to nothing under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Column text reflows with the viewport. Long unbreakable strings (URLs, code) inside columns may overflow; keep those out of .brutal-news-columns.
  • The lead's headline is the largest type in the template. Keep it under nine words so it holds two lines at 1280px; the overview's is six.
