Component

Hero

The opening of a page: a heading at the largest size, a paragraph, one or two buttons, and beside them whatever the template leads with. Every template has its own hero form; this page shows Brutal News's, then two variants that move.

Preview

The hero as the overview uses it. The heading takes --font-size-hero; the paragraph is muted and capped at a readable measure.

<section class="hero brutal-news-lead">
  <p class="kicker">Front page · The template</p>
  <h1>Set loud. Ruled thick. Never rounded.</h1>
  <div class="brutal-news-columns">
    <p class="brutal-news-dropcap">Brutal News is a newspaper that never learned to whisper. Every box is ruled in three pixels of ink, the headlines are Archivo Black at the largest size the page will hold, and the palette is what you find in a newsagent's window: hot red, acid yellow, cobalt, signal green. Shadows are hard offsets or nothing.</p>
    <p>Underneath the shouting it is the same template as every other one in retemplate: the same markup, the same components, the same two stylesheets and one small script. Swap <code>css/</code> and <code>assets/</code> for another template's and the pages change their clothes.</p>
    <p>What this one adds is the furniture of a paper. A masthead with a dateline. Story cards with kickers and bylines. A scoreboard table. A breaking banner. A pull quote set between rules. Datelines and data in JetBrains Mono, body in Inter, in real columns.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="row">
    <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
    <a class="btn btn-lg" href="index.html">Browse the components</a>
  </div>
</section>

Variants

Hot off the press

The headline slams onto the page in four hard steps, a red rule wipes across under it, and a stamp lands on the corner, tilted. Nothing eases: every move is stepped, like a press. Add brutal-news-press to the hero. Under prefers-reduced-motion the global rule cuts every animation to nothing, so the hero is simply there.

<section class="hero brutal-news-lead brutal-news-press">
  <span class="badge brutal-news-stamp">Stop press</span>
  <p class="kicker">Late edition</p>
  <h1>Slammed on. Ruled red. Stamped.</h1>
  <div class="brutal-news-columns">
    <p>The headline arrives in four steps, not a glide. The rule under it wipes left to right in six. The stamp lands last, at an angle, and stays.</p>
    <p>Everything else on the page is still. Under reduced motion all three are simply there.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="row">
    <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>
    <a class="btn btn-lg" href="index.html">Browse the components</a>
  </div>
</section>

The roller

A black roller crosses the hero from left to right and the headline is printed behind it: the text is clipped to the roller's trailing edge, so each letter appears the moment the ink passes. Runs once, on load. Add brutal-news-roller to the hero; the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.

<section class="hero brutal-news-lead brutal-news-roller">
  <p class="kicker">Printed as you watch</p>
  <h1>Ink goes on. Words come out.</h1>
  <div class="brutal-news-columns">
    <p>The roller is one black bar sliding across; the headline's clip follows its trailing edge. Two keyframes, one and a half seconds.</p>
    <p>The columns under it print after, in the same way, one beat later.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="row">
    <a class="btn btn-primary btn-lg" href="../index.html">Get started</a>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css: the hero under /* == utilities == */ with the other layout helpers, the variant under /* == hero variants == */.

ClassPurpose
.heroThe opening section: a grid of a text stack and, in most templates, a picture or a piece of flare
.hero .stackThe kicker, heading, paragraph and button row
.hero .rowThe buttons
.hero.brutal-news-pressThe stepped slam, the wipe and the stamp
.badge.brutal-news-stampThe red stamp pinned to the top corner
.hero.brutal-news-rollerThe roller bar and the printed headline and columns

Notes

  • One <h1> per page: the hero's heading is it. The kicker above it is a <p>, not a heading.
  • Above 48rem the hero is two columns, text then picture; below, it stacks. The variant keeps whatever layout its markup implies.
  • The variant is CSS only. Its animation is declared with both fill, so the page is correct before it starts and after it ends, and the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.
  • Another template ignores brutal-news-press and shows its own hero; any flare markup inside sits in the template's flare wrapper so a swap can drop it.
