Breaking

The council will publish the baths survey in full at 6pm tonight, four months after it was written and one day after the Bugle asked for it.

Council · Exclusive

Baths shut in March. Council knew in May.

Ashcombe Baths will close on 2 March for at least eighteen months, the council confirmed last night, after the Bugle obtained a structural survey describing the roof trusses as "beyond economic repair". The survey is dated 11 May. Councillors were briefed in June. The swimming club, which trains there five mornings a week, found out on Monday when a notice went up by the lockers.

The report, by engineers Harte and Pell, gives the building two winters at most and recommends the pool be drained before the second. Repair is costed at £4.1m; a new pool at Mill Field at £11m. The council has budgeted for neither.

Asked why the closure was not announced in June, the council's leader, Gwen Tallis, said the "options had to be understood first". Asked what the two hundred children in the swimming club would do, she said the club "had been made aware". The club told the Bugle it had not.

By Marta Okonkwo, chief reporter
