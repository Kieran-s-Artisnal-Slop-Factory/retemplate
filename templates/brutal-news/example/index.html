Bridge to close for a year. Nobody told the bus.

Mill Lane bridge will shut to traffic from October for twelve months of repairs, the county confirmed last night, after the Gazette obtained an engineer's report describing the deck as "at the end of its life". The number 4 bus, which crosses it eleven times a day, has not yet been given a diversion.

The report, dated June, was not published. Councillors were briefed in July. Residents found out on Tuesday, when a contractor's sign appeared on the parapet.
