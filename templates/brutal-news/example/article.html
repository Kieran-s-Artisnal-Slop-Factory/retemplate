Bridge to close for a year. Nobody told the bus.

Exclusive

Mill Lane bridge will shut to traffic from 6 October for twelve months of repairs, the county confirmed last night, after the Gazette obtained an engineer's report describing the deck as "at the end of its serviceable life". The number 4 bus, which crosses it eleven times a day and is the only route between North Millbrook and the station, has not yet been given a diversion.

The report, dated 14 June, was not published. Councillors were briefed on 9 July. Residents found out on Tuesday, when a contractor's sign appeared on the north parapet giving a start date and a phone number that rings out.

Asked why the closure had not been announced, a county spokesperson said the "communications plan was being finalised". Asked what the bus would do, they said the operator "had been informed". The operator told the Gazette it had not.

What closes, and when

  • 6 October: the bridge closes to all vehicles. Pedestrians and cyclists keep a fenced walkway on the downstream side.
  • November to March: the deck is removed and rebuilt. The walkway may close for up to two weeks during lifting.
  • Summer 2027: reopening, "subject to weather and the discovery of anything unexpected", which residents of a Victorian bridge will recognise as a large subject.

A closure everyone can see coming is not an emergency. It is a diary entry.

The bus

The number 4 is the only service across the river. Without a diversion, North Millbrook loses its link to the station, the surgery and the secondary school. The nearest alternative crossing adds twenty-two minutes by road.

The operator says it will "work with the county to agree a route" and expects an answer "in the coming weeks". The school term started on Tuesday.

In numbers

11 crossings a day · 1,400 passengers a week · 22 extra minutes by the next bridge · 0 diversions announced

What happens next

The county's cabinet meets on 18 September. The Gazette has asked for the engineer's report to be published in full, and will publish it ourselves if it is not.
