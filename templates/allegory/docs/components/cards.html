Cards

A bordered surface with optional media, a title and text. Vertical by default; horizontal with a class; and one feature card per template with an effect that suits the design.

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == cards == */.

ClassPurpose
.cardThe surface: border, radius, shadow, flex column
.card-mediaFigure holding an image or an inline SVG
.card-iconAn inline SVG sized and coloured as an icon
.card-bodyPadding and vertical spacing
.card-titleThe heading
.card-textMuted description
.card-actionsButtons at the bottom, pushed down
.card-horizontalMedia beside the body
.card-media-endWith .card-horizontal: media on the right
.card-featureThe template's dynamic card: turns over to the other face on hover and focus
.card.allegory-haloPinned to the day face, ornament at the foot (Allegory only)
.card.allegory-hornPinned to the night face, ornament at the foot (Allegory only)

Notes

  • Cards are <article> elements; use <div> if the content is not self-contained.
  • Lay them out with .grid-2 or .grid-3; they stretch to equal heights.
  • Horizontal cards stack on screens narrower than 30rem.
  • The feature card responds to :hover and :focus-within; it is CSS only. The card rotates half a turn on its vertical axis and its children mirror themselves with scaleX(-1), so the text reads correctly on the back; the colours swap at the midpoint, when the card is edge-on. The markup is the same as every other card.
  • The back is always the other face: the --allegory-face-other-* and --allegory-band-other tokens are the opposite halves of the page's own pairs, so the flip works whatever the scheme. Under prefers-reduced-motion the swap is instant.
  • The title of a feature card sits on the band (gold by day, red by night) at display size and weight. Keep it short; body text does not belong on a band.
