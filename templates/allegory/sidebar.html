The other layout

Two faces, one column

The overview again, in the sidebar layout the docs use. On a wide screen the sidebar is a column with a band along its edge; on a narrow one it is a drawer behind the menu button that closes on Esc or a tap outside. The diptych fits the narrower column because it is a grid, not a fixed width.

What the layout is made of

A grid with two rows and, above 64rem, two columns. The top bar spans both and stays put; the sidebar is a sticky column with its own scrollbar; the page scrolls under it. Below 64rem the sidebar carries the popover attribute, so the menu button is a real button that opens it, and the browser supplies the backdrop and the dismissal.

Groups fold

Each navigation group is a <details>, open by default. Close the ones you do not need.

Docs
Can the sidebar collapse on a wide screen?

Not in this version. The drawer is for narrow screens only; on a wide screen the column is always there.

Does the drawer remember being open?

No. It is a popover: it opens when asked and closes when dismissed, and there is nothing to remember.
