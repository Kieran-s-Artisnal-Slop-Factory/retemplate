Open seven days a week

A quiet room, a warm chair, and every book in the county.

Millbrook Public Library lends books, tools, and time. Membership is free to anyone who lives, works or studies in the district.

Join the library This month's events

What we lend

Books, obviously

Forty thousand titles on the shelves and anything else through the county request service, usually within a week.

Tools and instruments

Drills, sewing machines, a telescope, two ukuleles. The library of things lives on the ground floor.

Rooms and desks

Book the study room for up to three hours, or take any desk with a view of the river, first come first served.

Visit

12 Mill Lane, by the bridge

Step-free from the street, with the children's room on the ground floor and the reading room upstairs. Bikes lock up outside; the number 4 bus stops at the door.

Opening hours

Questions people ask at the desk

How many books can I borrow?

Twenty at a time, for three weeks, renewable twice unless someone else is waiting.

What if I return something late?

Nothing happens. We stopped charging fines in 2023 and the books came back anyway.

Can I use the library without joining?

Yes. Reading, studying, the toilets and the Wi-Fi are for everyone. Borrowing needs a card, which takes two minutes.
