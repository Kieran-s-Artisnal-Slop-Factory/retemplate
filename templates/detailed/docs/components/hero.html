Hero

The opening of a page: a heading at the largest size, a paragraph, one or two buttons, and beside them whatever the template leads with. Every template has its own hero form; this page shows Detailed's, then a second variant that moves.

Variants

The curtain

Two pleated curtains in the band colour, each edged in gold, part from the centre and slide off to either side, and the marquee is there behind them with its letters spreading to their full tracking. It plays once, on load. Add detailed-curtain to the hero. Under prefers-reduced-motion the global rule cuts every animation to nothing, so the hero is simply there.

<section class="hero detailed-marquee detailed-curtain">
  <div class="stack">

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css: the hero under /* == utilities == */ with the other layout helpers, the variant under /* == hero variants == */.

ClassPurpose
.heroThe opening section: a grid of a text stack and, in most templates, a picture or a piece of flare
.hero .stackThe kicker, heading, paragraph and button row
.hero .rowThe buttons
.hero.detailed-marquee.detailed-curtainThe marquee with the curtains drawn over it, parting on load

Notes

  • One <h1> per page: the hero's heading is it. The kicker above it is a <p>, not a heading.
  • Above 48rem the hero is two columns, text then picture; below, it stacks. The variant keeps whatever layout its markup implies.
  • The variant is CSS only. Its animation is declared with both fill, so the page is correct before it starts and after it ends, and the global prefers-reduced-motion rule stops it.
  • Another template ignores detailed-curtain and shows its own hero; any flare markup inside sits in the template's flare wrapper so a swap can drop it.
