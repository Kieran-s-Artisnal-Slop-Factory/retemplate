Flare

Flare is everything a template adds beyond the contract. Detailed adds a sunburst drawn as inline SVG, a stepped frame with a title plate, the chevron ornament as a block, and one hero variant that puts them together. The rays behind every page are not flare; they are tokens on <body> and need no markup. The variants of contract components (the stepped card, the gold outline button, the menu table) are documented on those components' pages and listed at the bottom of this one.

Sunburst

A half-disc of rays and three arcs, stroked with the current colour, which the class sets to the ornament gold. It scales to its container and keeps its proportions. On its own it is a section header; inside .hero.detailed-marquee it sits behind the heading.

Stepped frame

A panel clipped to the step polygon, with a double gold rule inside and a title plate: a bronze hexagon in tracked capitals. Headings inside it set in Limelight. Use it for the one thing on a page that should read as an announcement: the hours, the house rules, a reservation form.

Chevron

The page-break ornament as a free-standing block, a little larger. It is the same --detailed-chevron mask filled with --detailed-gold, so it recolours with the scheme. Use <hr class="page-break"> when you mean a break between sections; use this when you only want the drawing.

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == flare == */. The last four rows are variants of contract components; their rules are in the same section and their examples on the pages named.

ClassPurpose
.detailed-flareThe wrapper for flare that is markup; strip it when swapping templates
.detailed-sunburstGold fan of rays; an inline SVG scaled to its container
.detailed-marqueeOn .hero: one centred column, the sunburst behind the heading
.detailed-frameStepped panel with a double gold rule; .detailed-frame-title is the plate
.detailed-chevronThe chevron mask as a free-standing block
.card.detailed-steppedStepped top corners with a gold hairline (cards)
.btn.detailed-outlineGold double outline, fills gold on hover (buttons)
.table-wrap.detailed-menuA table set like a bill of fare; tr.detailed-course is a course heading (table)

Notes

  • Every colour here is a token: --detailed-gold, --detailed-gold-soft, --detailed-brass, --detailed-band and the rest in theme.css. Nothing in global.css names a colour.
  • Nothing animates continuously. The only motion in the template is the sheen on the feature card, which runs once per hover and is removed under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • The sunburst and the chevron are decoration; mark them aria-hidden="true" as the examples do. The frame is content and should not be.
  • The rays behind every page (--detailed-rays, --detailed-rays-fade) are background layers on body, documented on the theme page. Remove the background-image line in global.css to lose them.
