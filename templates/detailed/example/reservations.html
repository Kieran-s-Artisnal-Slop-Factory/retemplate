The rooms

Quay room

Floors one to three, facing the quay or the courtyard. A king bed or two singles, a walk-in shower, a desk by the window.

River suite

Floors four to eight. A sitting room with the river in both windows, a bedroom behind a walnut screen, a bath you can lie down in.

Tower suite

Two only, under the lantern on the ninth floor, with the terrace between them. The whole quay, the bridge and the estuary, and the original 1931 bathroom.

The house rules, briefly

Cancelling

Free until noon two days before you arrive. After that, the first night. If the river floods and the trains stop, none of this applies.

Children

Welcome everywhere, including the Lantern Room until eight. Cots are free; a folding bed for an older child is £30 a night in the suites.

Paying

On the morning you leave, by card or by cash. We do not take a deposit for stays under four nights.
