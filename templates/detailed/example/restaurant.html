The kitchen

Tomas Kell cooks what the day brings

Tomas has run the Lantern Room since 2019. Before that he cooked on the quay itself, in the café the fishermen used, which is where the oysters and the eel come from and why the boat names are on the menu when we know them.

Beyond the menu

The tasting menu

Seven courses, decided at five o'clock, served to the whole table from seven. Seventy-five a head; the wine pairing is forty more and is chosen by Priya Sethi, who keeps the cellar.

Tonight's pairings

A dry fino with the oysters; a Loire chenin with the eel and the leeks; a village Chablis with the turbot; a Rhône syrah with the lamb; a late-harvest riesling with the tart; a tawny port with the cheese. Priya will swap any glass for one you would rather have.

The private room

The old boardroom, seating fourteen at the original table. Its own door from the hall, its own waiter, and the tasting menu or a menu agreed with Tomas a week ahead.

Breakfast

Seven until half past ten in the Lantern Room, for residents and anyone else. Kippers, eggs from the walled garden, and the tart if there is any left.
