The building

Nine floors of walnut and brass

The Marlowe opened in the spring of 1931 as the shipping offices of Marlowe & Daughters, and became a hotel when the ships stopped coming. The lift cages, the sunburst over the door and the terrazzo in the hall are the originals. The plumbing is not.

Questions guests ask the desk

When can I arrive, and when must I leave?

Rooms are ready from three in the afternoon and are yours until eleven the next morning. If you arrive early we will take your bags and give you a chair in the hall; if you need to leave late, ask the night before and we will usually say yes.

Is there parking?

Eight spaces under the arches on Wharf Lane, first come first served, and the public garage on Regent Street for the rest. The porter will move your car for you either way.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, to the quay rooms and the bar, and not to the Lantern Room. There is a bowl, a bed and a map of the river walk waiting in the room.
