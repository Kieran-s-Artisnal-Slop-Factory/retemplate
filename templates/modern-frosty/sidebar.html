All templates

The overview, with a sidebar

Every template ships two layouts. The navbar layout is on the overview, the blog post and the forms page; this page and the whole docs tree use the sidebar layout. The top bar is frosted like the navbar; the sidebar is a plain column on the page colour, ruled off by one hairline. On a narrow screen it becomes a frosted drawer behind the menu button and closes on Esc or a tap outside.

What the layout is made of

A grid with two rows and, above 64rem, two columns. The top bar spans both columns and stays put; the sidebar is a sticky column with its own scrollbar; the page scrolls under the bar. Below 64rem the sidebar carries the popover attribute, so the menu button is a real button that opens it, and the browser supplies the frosted backdrop and the dismiss behaviour.

Groups fold

Each navigation group is a <details>, open by default, with a mono label. Close the ones you do not need.

The current page

The link to the page you are on carries aria-current="page", which is also what draws the blue rule beside it.

Same content, either way

Nothing on this page needs the sidebar. Swap the shell and the page still works.

How it is built
Can the sidebar collapse on a wide screen?

Not in this version. The drawer behaviour is for narrow screens only; on a wide screen the column is always there.

Does the drawer remember being open?

No. It is a popover: it opens when asked and closes when dismissed, and there is nothing to remember.
