Three things it does not do

Most note apps add. Sheaf leaves things out on purpose, and the three below are the ones people ask about.

No database

Every note is a Markdown file in a folder on your disk. Open the folder in anything else and the notes are all there.

No folders to fill

Notes link to each other by name. Type two brackets and the name, and the link is made; the other note lists it under linked from.

No save button

The file is written as you type and the toolbar says saved when it is. Sync to your other devices happens on the same tick.

One window, two panes

The list on the left, the note on the right, and a search box above both. There is no third pane, no graph view and no dashboard. Anything you need to find, you type.

Before you sign up

Where do the files live?

In a folder you choose the first time you open Sheaf. On the free plan that folder syncs between two devices; on the paid plans, any number.

Can I leave?

At any moment. Your notes are already a folder of Markdown files; stop paying and the folder stays on your disk.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Notes are read and written locally. Sync catches up when you are back on a network.
