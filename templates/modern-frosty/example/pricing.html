Questions about plans

What happens to my notes if I stop paying?

Nothing. They are files in a folder on your disk. Sync goes back to two devices, and shared folders become read only until someone on the team is paying again.

Is there a student price?

Personal is half price with a school email. Write to us from that address and we will set it up.

How is a seat counted?

A seat is one person who can write in the team's shared folders. People who only read do not need a seat.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. A year of Personal costs ten months; a year of Team costs ten months a seat.
