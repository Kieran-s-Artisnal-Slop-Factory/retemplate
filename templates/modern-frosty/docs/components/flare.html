Flare

Flare is everything a template adds beyond the contract. Modern Frosty adds the note, the ink slab with its command line and live dot, and a short list of keyboard shortcuts; the dock (the floating navbar) and the serif counterpoint need no markup of their own. Its five variants of contract components (the ledger, the quiet button, the lined accordion group, the inline field and the plans table) are documented on those components' pages and listed at the bottom of this one.

The note

A piece of the example app: a frosted toolbar over a ruled sheet. The toolbar is position: sticky inside the note, so when the note scrolls the lines pass under it and the frost has something to blur. The lines are a repeating gradient of one hairline colour, so they swap with the scheme. Use it as a hero, as the overview and the example home do, or anywhere a page wants to show the product rather than describe it.

<div class="modern-frosty-flare">
  <div class="modern-frosty-note">
    <div class="modern-frosty-toolbar"><b>Reading list</b><span>edited today</span><span>saved</span></div>
    <div class="modern-frosty-sheet">
      <h2>Things to read this week</h2>
      <p>Two papers, one long essay and the <mark>chapter</mark> I keep putting off.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>The essay first. It is shortest.</li>
        <li>The chapter on Thursday, on paper.</li>
      </ul>
      <p class="muted">Notes link when you type two brackets.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

A scrolling note

Add modern-frosty-scroll and the note is capped at 22rem and scrolls inside itself, which is what gives the toolbar its frost. The overview's hero is one of these; scroll it and watch the lines pass under the bar.

<div class="modern-frosty-flare">
  <div class="modern-frosty-note modern-frosty-scroll">
    <div class="modern-frosty-toolbar"><b>Meeting notes</b><span>2 people</span><span>saved</span></div>
    <div class="modern-frosty-sheet">
      <h2>Tuesday</h2>
      <p>Agreed to move the release to the second week. The import tool needs another pass; the export is done.</p>
      <p>Open questions: whether the free plan keeps sync, and who writes the changelog.</p>
      <p>Actions: draft the pricing page, ask about the domain, book the room for Thursday.</p>
      <p class="muted">Keep scrolling. The toolbar stays where it is.</p>
      <p>The lines here are one repeated hairline; the toolbar is the same frost as the navbar, so the two read as the same material.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

The ink slab

The one inverted surface: the text colour as ground, the ground colour as text, so it reads as the page turned over. A section with modern-frosty-slab loses its top rule and gains the inversion; buttons inside it invert too. The command line is a <p> with a prompt, a <code> and a caret that blinks, and the live dot is a span before a kicker with a ring that pings.

<div class="modern-frosty-flare">
  <div class="modern-frosty-slab">
    <p class="kicker"><span class="modern-frosty-live"></span>Now on every plan</p>
    <h2>Sync, <em>quietly.</em></h2>
    <p>Notes are files in a folder you own. Sync copies them; it never rewrites them.</p>
    <p class="modern-frosty-command"><span>$</span><code>sheaf sync --watch</code><span class="modern-frosty-caret"></span></p>
  </div>
</div>

Keys

A wrapping list of shortcuts, each a <kbd> and a word. The features page of the example uses it under the table of commands.

<div class="modern-frosty-flare">
  <ul class="modern-frosty-keys">
    <li><kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd> new note</li>
    <li><kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> find</li>
    <li><kbd>[[</kbd> link a note</li>
    <li><kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>.</kbd> toggle the sidebar</li>
  </ul>
</div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == flare == */. The last five rows are variants of contract components; their rules are in the component's own section and their examples on the pages named.

ClassPurpose
.modern-frosty-flareThe wrapper for flare that is markup; strip it when swapping templates
.modern-frosty-noteThe bordered note: a toolbar and a sheet
.modern-frosty-toolbarThe frosted, sticky bar; b is the title, the last span is pushed right
.modern-frosty-sheetRuled lines at 1.75rem; headings and paragraphs sit on the lines
.modern-frosty-scrollCaps the note at 22rem and lets it scroll
.modern-frosty-keysA wrapping list of shortcuts
.modern-frosty-slabThe inverted surface; on a .section it also drops the top rule
.modern-frosty-commandA command line: prompt span, code, caret
.modern-frosty-caretThe blinking caret
.modern-frosty-liveThe ember dot with a pinging ring, before a kicker
.modern-frosty-serif, h1 em, h2 emThe Newsreader italic counterpoint
.grid-2.modern-frosty-ledger, .grid-3.modern-frosty-ledgerCards sharing hairlines (cards)
.btn.modern-frosty-quietThe text button (buttons)
.accordion-group.modern-frosty-linesThe unboxed group (accordion)
.field.modern-frosty-inlineLabel beside the control (forms)
.table-wrap.modern-frosty-plans, .modern-frosty-pickThe plans table and its picked column (table)

Notes

  • The frost is two tokens, --modern-frosty-frost (the fill) and --modern-frosty-blur (8px), used by the navbar, the top bar, the toolbar, the dialog, the lightbox controls, the mobile menu, the drawer and the feature card. Lower the blur in theme.css and every one of them follows.
  • Motion is small and named: the hero rises once on load, the note drifts, the caret blinks and the live dot pings. Everything else is a hover colour or the feature card's frost clearing, and the global prefers-reduced-motion rule shortens all of it to nothing.
  • The dock is the navbar with no extra markup: it floats var(--space-3) from the top, is capped at the page width, and is the only thing on an ordinary page with a shadow.
  • Flare must still use tokens: no colour literals in global.css. The ice tint and the rule colour are --modern-frosty-ice and --modern-frosty-rule in theme.css.
  • The variants need no wrapper: another template ignores the class and shows the plain component. Only the note and the keys are markup to strip, and they sit in .modern-frosty-flare.
