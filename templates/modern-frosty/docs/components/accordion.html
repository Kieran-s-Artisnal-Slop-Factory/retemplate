Component

Accordion

A disclosure with a title and a body. Alone it is a <details> with a class; several with a shared name become an exclusive group.

Preview

<details class="accordion">
  <summary>How does it open?</summary>
  <div class="accordion-body"><p>It is a native <code>&lt;details&gt;</code> element. The browser handles open and closed, and the summary is keyboard-focusable on its own.</p></div>
</details>

Variants

Exclusive group

Wrap accordions in .accordion-group and give them the same name. The browser keeps at most one open.

<div class="accordion-group">
  <details class="accordion" name="group-a" open>
    <summary>First</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body"><p>Open by default.</p></div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion" name="group-a">
    <summary>Second</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body"><p>Opening this closes the first.</p></div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion" name="group-a">
    <summary>Third</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body"><p>And this closes whichever was open.</p></div>
  </details>
</div>

Lines

Add modern-frosty-lines to a group and it loses its box: no border, no fill, only the hairlines above, between and below the items, with the summaries flush left. For questions inside a page of text, where a box would read as a card. Works with or without name.

<div class="accordion-group modern-frosty-lines">
  <details class="accordion" name="group-b" open>
    <summary>Does the free plan sync?</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body"><p>Yes, between two devices.</p></div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion" name="group-b">
    <summary>Can I export my notes?</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body"><p>As Markdown files in a folder, at any time, from any plan.</p></div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion" name="group-b">
    <summary>Is there a desktop app?</summary>
    <div class="accordion-body"><p>Not yet. The web app installs as one.</p></div>
  </details>
</div>

Classes

The rules live in css/global.css under /* == accordion == */.

ClassPurpose
.accordionThe details element: border, background, padding
summaryThe clickable title; the marker is a plus that rotates
.accordion-bodyPadding around the content
.accordion-groupJoins several into one bordered stack; hosts the exclusive name
.modern-frosty-linesModern Frosty only, on the group: no box, hairlines between items

Notes

  • No script. <details name> is what makes a group exclusive.
  • Keyboard: Tab to the summary, Enter or Space to toggle.
  • Put open on one accordion to start it open.
